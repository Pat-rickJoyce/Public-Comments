# Comment Analysis: CMS-2026-1255-0001

---

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** VillageMD (representing Village Medical, Summit Health, CityMD, Starling Physicians)
- **Stakeholder type:** Healthcare Provider
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Business support services organization to physician-led independent provider groups across primary care, specialty care, and urgent care; experience in value-based care contracts including MSSP and ACO REACH; serves rural, suburban, and urban areas including medically underserved areas

---

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

VillageMD strongly supports CMS's proposed expansion of electronic prior authorization to drugs and standardization across payers, emphasizing that reforms will reduce administrative friction and improve patient outcomes. The organization recommends strengthening enforcement with clear penalties for noncompliance and further aligning standards across Medicare, Medicaid, CHIP, and Marketplace programs to reduce provider complexity.

---

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL, PA-DRUG-PHARMACY, FHIR-IG-REQUIRED, PA-DENIAL-REASONS, PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS, API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** Medicare Advantage Star Ratings, Qualified Health Plan oversight, Medicare Shared Savings Program (MSSP), ACO REACH
- **Standards referenced:** FHIR-based APIs, Prior Authorization APIs, electronic prior authorization, standardized interoperability frameworks

---

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Electronic Prior Authorization Expansion to Drugs**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL, PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Expansion requires payers to incorporate drug coverage and documentation requirements into PA APIs, significantly streamlining clinician workflows by reducing manual processes. Addresses long-standing inefficiencies that delay patient care and burden providers.
- **Specific request:** Finalize expansion of electronic PA to drugs as proposed.

**Topic 2: Standardized Interoperability Frameworks (FHIR APIs)**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** FHIR-based APIs enable more efficient, real-time communication between providers and payers while reducing duplicative documentation requirements.
- **Specific request:** Adopt standardized FHIR-based APIs as proposed.

**Topic 3: Shortened and Standardized Decision Timeframes**
- **Provision ID:** PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Will reduce delays in patient access to critical medications and create more predictable and reliable provider workflows.
- **Specific request:** Establish shortened and standardized decision timeframes as proposed.

**Topic 4: Transparency Requirements (Public Reporting and Denial Reasons)**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS, API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Public reporting of PA metrics and clearer explanations for denials improve accountability and enable providers to more effectively support patients through appeals and resubmissions.
- **Specific request:** Finalize enhanced transparency requirements.

**Topic 5: Enforcement and Penalties**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify/Strengthen
- **Rationale:** Historically, variability in payer compliance has limited effectiveness of PA reforms. Without robust enforcement, benefits of standardization and interoperability may not be fully realized, and provider burden may persist.
- **Specific request:** (1) Establish clear, enforceable penalties for noncompliance including financial consequences tied to failure to meet decision timelines, failure to provide specific denial reasons, and failure to maintain functional APIs; (2) Publicly report plan-level compliance performance including violations and corrective actions; (3) Incorporate compliance metrics into Medicare Advantage Star Ratings and QHP oversight.

**Topic 6: Cross-Program Alignment**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify/Expand
- **Rationale:** Organizations serving patients across multiple coverage types face administrative complexity from inconsistent standards. Greater consistency needed in decision timeframes, electronic PA standards across medical/pharmacy benefits, and reporting requirements.
- **Specific request:** (1) Fully harmonize decision timeframes across programs, particularly aligning MA and Marketplace requirements more closely with Medicaid's more stringent timelines for certain drug classes; (2) Ensure consistency in application of electronic PA standards across medical and pharmacy benefits; (3) Align reporting requirements and performance metrics across all payers.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Finalize expansion of electronic PA requirements to drugs
2. Adopt standardized FHIR-based APIs
3. Establish shortened and standardized decision timeframes
4. Require public reporting of PA metrics and clear denial explanations
5. Establish clear, enforceable penalties for noncompliance tied to decision timeline failures, inadequate denial reasons, and non-functional APIs
6. Publicly report plan-level compliance performance including violations and corrective actions
7. Incorporate compliance metrics into Medicare Advantage Star Ratings and QHP oversight
8. Fully harmonize decision timeframes across Medicare, Medicaid, CHIP, and Marketplace programs
9. Align MA and Marketplace requirements more closely with Medicaid's stringent timelines for certain drug classes
10. Ensure consistency in electronic PA standards across medical and pharmacy benefits
11. Align reporting requirements and performance metrics across all payers
