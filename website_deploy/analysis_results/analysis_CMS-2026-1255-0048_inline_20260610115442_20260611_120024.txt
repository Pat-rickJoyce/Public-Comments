## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The commenter urges CMS to protect oncology patients from delays and denials caused by rigid prior authorization requirements for laboratory and biomarker testing. She recommends retroactive authorization after specimen collection, prohibiting denials based only on authorization timing, avoiding repeat blood draws, expedited review for oncology diagnostic and biomarker testing, and patient protection from surprise administrative costs.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** GENERAL/RFI; PATIENT-ACCESS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS by analogy.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** CMS-0062-P; Section III.E.
- **Standards referenced:** None.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Oncology laboratory and biomarker testing**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Biomarker and specialty laboratory results guide cancer treatment selection, treatment changes, monitoring, and clinical trial eligibility.
- **Specific request:** Protect oncology patients from PA delays and denials for medically necessary lab and biomarker testing.

**Topic 2: Retroactive authorization and specimen timing**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Authorization-before-collection rules can cause valid specimens to be delayed, rejected, or discarded even when parties acted in good faith.
- **Specific request:** Allow retroactive authorization after specimen collection and prohibit denials solely based on authorization timing.

**Topic 3: Repeat blood draws and expedited review**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Repeat blood draws and delayed biomarker review can delay urgent cancer care.
- **Specific request:** Prevent unnecessary repeat blood draws and require expedited review for oncology-related diagnostic and biomarker testing.

**Topic 4: Surprise costs**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Patients should not bear costs caused by administrative timing issues.
- **Specific request:** Protect patients from surprise costs when delays are administrative.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S66-S82:** The comment directly responds to CMS's laboratory testing RFI by describing timing barriers for oncology biomarker testing and proposing specific protections.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Allow retroactive authorization when specimens have already been collected.
2. Prohibit denials based solely on authorization timing.
3. Prevent unnecessary repeat blood draws when valid specimens are available.
4. Require expedited review for oncology-related diagnostic and biomarker testing.
5. Protect patients from surprise costs caused by administrative delays.
