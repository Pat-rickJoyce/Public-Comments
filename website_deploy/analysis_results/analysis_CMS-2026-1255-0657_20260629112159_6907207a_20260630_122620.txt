## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The commenter urges CMS to protect oncology patients from prior authorization delays and denials for medically necessary laboratory and biomarker testing. The comment supports PA modernization but asks CMS to require retroactive authorization, prohibit timing-only denials, avoid repeat blood draws, expedite oncology lab review, and protect patients from surprise costs caused by administrative delays.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** GENERAL/RFI on prior authorization and access to laboratory testing; related to PATIENT-ACCESS concerns. No direct response to drug PA API provisions.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** Section III.E of CMS-0062-P; no CFR sections cited.
- **Standards referenced:** Not addressed.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Prior authorization for oncology laboratory and biomarker testing**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL/RFI
- **Position:** Modify.
- **Rationale:** Biomarker and specialty lab results guide treatment selection, treatment changes, disease monitoring, and clinical trial eligibility. PA timing rules can delay, reject, or discard valid specimens and force repeat blood draws.
- **Specific request:** Require plans to allow retroactive authorization, prohibit denials based solely on authorization timing, prevent unnecessary repeat blood draws, expedite oncology lab reviews, and protect patients from surprise administrative costs.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S66-S82:** Responds to the RFI on prior authorization and access to laboratory testing. The commenter asks CMS to prevent administrative PA timing requirements from delaying oncology diagnosis and treatment decisions.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Allow retroactive authorization when laboratory specimens have already been collected.
2. Prohibit denials based solely on authorization timing.
3. Prevent unnecessary repeat blood draws when a valid specimen is available.
4. Require expedited review for oncology-related diagnostic and biomarker testing.
5. Protect patients from surprise costs when delays are administrative.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Rigid PA requirements for laboratory and biomarker testing can delay or deny critical cancer care.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Valid specimens may be delayed, rejected, or discarded; patients may face repeat blood draws, surprise costs, and delayed treatment decisions.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Authorization timing rules tied to specimen collection dates.
