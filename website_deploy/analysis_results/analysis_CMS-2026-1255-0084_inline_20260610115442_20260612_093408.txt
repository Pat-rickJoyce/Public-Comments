## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Dr. Starling strongly supports CMS-0062-P but urges CMS to strengthen protections for repeat reauthorization, step therapy, and continuity of care. The comment argues that stable patients receiving effective CGRP-targeting therapy should not lose access because of arbitrary administrative reauthorization or coverage criteria inconsistent with current clinical guidelines.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS; GENERAL
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** CMS-0062-P; no CFR sections cited.
- **Standards referenced:** American Headache Society and American Academy of Neurology clinical guidance; no FHIR IG or NCPDP technical standard named.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Continuity of care and repeat reauthorization**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Repeat reauthorization can disrupt effective treatment for stable patients despite documented clinical response.
- **Specific request:** Establish enforceable continuity-of-care protections and prevent arbitrary re-justification requirements for stable patients.

**Topic 2: Step therapy reform**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Fail-first requirements may force use of contraindicated, previously failed, or medically inappropriate medications, delaying care and prolonging disability.
- **Specific request:** Strengthen the rule with meaningful step therapy reform.

**Topic 3: Evidence-based coverage criteria**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Coverage criteria should align with specialty clinical guidance and not contradict accepted migraine treatment standards.
- **Specific request:** Require coverage criteria to reflect American Headache Society and American Academy of Neurology guidance.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S16:** The comment indirectly responds by calling for denial and reauthorization criteria that reflect clinical reality and documented benefit.
- **S20:** The comment addresses the harms of delayed PA and reauthorization, including urgent and emergency care use.
- **S66-S82:** The comment raises general utilization management issues: repeat reauthorization, continuity of care, step therapy, and clinical guideline alignment.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Finalize the proposed rule.
2. Add strong, enforceable continuity-of-care protections.
3. Prevent repeat reauthorization for stable patients with documented clinical response.
4. Reform step therapy requirements that force clinically inappropriate medication trials.
5. Require coverage criteria to align with current evidence-based guidance from AHS and AAN.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Administrative reauthorization and step therapy disrupt effective treatment for stable migraine patients.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Return of illness, urgent care and emergency department visits, prolonged disability, career disruption, and progression from episodic to chronic migraine.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Burdensome repeat PA, forced medication switching, fail-first requirements, and coverage criteria misaligned with clinical evidence.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** Patient's headache frequency and symptom burden decreased by more than 75% on CGRP therapy; after denial, she had two urgent/emergency visits within three to four months.
- **Studies or research referenced:** AHS and AAN clinical guidance referenced generally; no specific publication cited.
- **Examples provided:** A 68-year-old Medicare beneficiary with chronic migraine returned to work after CGRP therapy, then worsened after repeat PA delayed and denied the effective medication.
