## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

MS Medicaid requests that CMS extend the implementation deadline to three years after the final rule's effective date for state Medicaid and CHIP programs, with an extension request process. The commenter cites workforce and budget strain from concurrent large IT projects, especially the NCPDP Telecommunication Standard update from D.0 to F6 by August 14, 2027.

- **Position:** Modify.

- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY

- **Rationale:** The NCPDP Telecommunication Standard update from D.0 to F6 due August 14, 2027 is a major concurrent IT project that strains state resources.
