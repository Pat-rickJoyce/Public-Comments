# Comment Analysis Template: CMS-2026-1255-0001

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Black Women's Health Imperative (BWHI); signed by Ifeoma C. Udoh, PhD, Executive VP: Policy, Advocacy and Science.
- **Stakeholder type:** Advocacy Group.
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** National organization dedicated to advancing the health and wellness of Black women and girls; cites CDC information on Black maternal mortality and cancer disparities.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

BWHI strongly supports extending electronic prior authorization for drugs across impacted coverage programs, but emphasizes that modernization must include accountability, equity, clear denial explanations, public metrics, and continuity-of-care safeguards. The commenter frames PA delays as a health equity issue for Black women managing chronic disease, pregnancy/postpartum health, cancer, and complex conditions.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; PA-DENIAL-REASONS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS; API-USAGE-METRICS; GENERAL/RFI on step therapy; PATIENT-ACCESS.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** No CFR sections cited.
- **Standards referenced:** Electronic prior authorization and interoperable systems generally; no specific FHIR IGs or NCPDP standards named.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Extend electronic prior authorization for drugs**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Support.
- **Rationale:** Outdated paper and fax processes burden providers, leave patients without timely information, and delay medically necessary medications.
- **Specific request:** Finalize ePA expansion across Medicare Advantage, Medicaid managed care, state Medicaid agencies, CHIP, and QHPs.

**Topic 2: Accountability for ePA implementation**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL; API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Position:** Modify / strengthen.
- **Rationale:** Faster systems alone will not resolve inequities if denials remain inconsistent or inappropriate.
- **Specific request:** Apply strong CMS oversight so electronic PA improves access rather than merely accelerating unjustified denials.

**Topic 3: Drug PA decision timelines**
- **Provision ID:** PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS
- **Position:** Support.
- **Rationale:** Even a few days of delay can cause missed doses, worsening symptoms, more appointments, and emotional/financial strain.
- **Specific request:** Apply 24-hour urgent and 72-hour standard protections consistently across affected coverage programs; monitor compliance and publicly evaluate access effects.

**Topic 4: Denial transparency**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS
- **Position:** Strengthen.
- **Rationale:** Black women too often receive vague or incomplete denial notices that do not support informed decisions, appeals, or provider resubmissions.
- **Specific request:** Require clear, specific, patient-accessible explanations for all PA denials, including initial denials, delivered timely to patients and providers.

**Topic 5: Equity-focused public reporting and step therapy continuity**
- **Provision ID:** API-USAGE-METRICS; GENERAL/RFI on step therapy
- **Position:** Modify / strengthen.
- **Rationale:** Public data should identify whether specific populations disproportionately experience delays, denials, or step therapy requirements; step therapy repetition after plan changes undermines continuity.
- **Specific request:** Increase public reporting at a level useful to patients/caregivers; require plans to honor previously satisfied step therapy requirements, disclose rules, and maintain transparent exceptions/appeals.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S14/S12:** Supports extending ePA for drugs across coverage programs to replace outdated paper/fax processes.
- **S16/S17:** Requests clear, specific, patient-accessible denial explanations for all PA denials, including initial denials.
- **S20/S43:** Supports 24-hour urgent and 72-hour standard drug PA timelines and consistent application across programs.
- **S29/S35:** Encourages public reporting of PA metrics to identify disproportionate delays, denials, or step therapy effects.
- **S66-S82:** Responds to step therapy RFI by urging continuity protections, honoring prior steps, clear disclosure, and accessible appeals/exceptions.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Finalize ePA expansion for drugs across MA, Medicaid managed care, state Medicaid, CHIP, and QHPs.
2. Pair modernization with strong CMS oversight and accountability.
3. Apply 24-hour urgent and 72-hour standard drug PA timelines consistently across affected programs.
4. Monitor compliance and publicly evaluate whether timelines improve access and continuity.
5. Require clear, specific, patient-accessible denial explanations for all PA denials.
6. Provide denial explanations to both patients and providers in a timely manner.
7. Publicly report metrics capable of identifying population-level disparities in PA delays, denials, and step therapy.
8. Require plans to honor previously satisfied step therapy requirements across transitions.
9. Require clear step therapy disclosure and transparent exceptions/appeals pathways.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** PA delays and denials contributing to disparities; vague denial notices; unjustified denials; step therapy repetition after coverage changes.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Treatment delays, interruptions, out-of-pocket costs, medical debt, eroded trust, missed doses, worsening symptoms, additional appointments, and harm during pregnancy/postpartum, cancer treatment, and chronic disease management.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Outdated paper/fax workflows, inconsistent denials, lack of patient-accessible communication, and coverage instability.
