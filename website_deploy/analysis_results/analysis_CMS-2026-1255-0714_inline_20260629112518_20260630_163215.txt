ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
Thank you for this public comment period, this is unquestionably one of the biggest issues facing patients and providers alike right now. I am a practicing pain and headache specialist at UCSD and treasurer of the American Academy of Pain Medicine. The current prior authorization burdens harm my patients with Medicare as well as contributing to healthcare provider burnout. On an almost daily basis I see patients who were previously stable on a treatment but are now unable to get that treatment--most often botulinumtoxin for chronic migraine--and their quality of life and function is adversely affected by this. Meanwhile, my entire team at UCSD is spending more and more time every year dealing with appealing prior authorization denials, especially from Medicare. Please fix this broken system that is causing so trouble for both patients and providers!...
