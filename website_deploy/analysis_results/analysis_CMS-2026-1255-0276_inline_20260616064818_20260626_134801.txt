ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
I am writing to express my support for the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) proposed rule to improve electronic prior authorization and prescription drug access. As a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner, I see firsthand how delays in medication approval can negatively impact patient outcomes and disrupt continuity of care.
Patients with depression, anxiety disorders, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, ADHD, and substance use disorders often depend on timely access to medications to maintain stability and prevent worsening symptoms. Unfortunately, prior authorization requirements frequently create barriers that delay treatment, increase provider administrative burden, and contribute to patient frustration and nonadherence.
The proposed CMS rule would modernize the prior authorization process by promoting electronic submission and improving communication between providers and health plans. These changes could reduce delays, increase transparency, and improve patient access to medically necessary treatments.
I strongly support policies that streamline prior authorization while maintaining appropriate safeguards for patient safety. Behavioral health patients are particularly vulnerable to treatment interruptions, and reducing unnecessary administrative barriers can improve outcomes, reduce hospitalizations, and enhance overall quality of care.
