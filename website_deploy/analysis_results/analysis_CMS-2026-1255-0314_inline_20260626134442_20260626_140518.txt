## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This brief inline comment makes four patient-focused recommendations related to prior authorization for laboratory and diagnostic tests, specifically calling for prohibition of timing-based denials, elimination of redundant blood draws, mandatory expedited review for oncology tests, and protection against surprise bills due to PA paperwork delays.

---

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** GENERAL (RFI on laboratory tests and DMEPOS)
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** CMS-2026-1255 (docket number only)
- **Standards referenced:** None

---

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Timing-Based Denials for Laboratory Tests**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify (additional requirement)
- **Rationale:** Current practice allows insurers to deny diagnostic tests solely because PA was still pending when a specimen was drawn, creating retroactive denials for good-faith care.
- **Specific request:** Prevent insurers from denying a critical test merely because authorization was still pending on the day the blood was drawn.

**Topic 2: Eliminate Redundant Blood Draws**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify (additional requirement)
- **Rationale:** Patients are sometimes forced to undergo repeat blood draws for tests already collected, causing unnecessary pain and inconvenience when a valid specimen is available.
- **Specific request:** Protect patients from being forced to undergo redundant procedures when a valid specimen already exists.

**Topic 3: Fast-Track Cancer Care**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify (additional requirement)
- **Rationale:** Oncology and other time-sensitive diagnostic tests require urgency that standard PA processes do not accommodate.
- **Specific request:** Establish mandatory expedited review pathways for oncology and other time-sensitive diagnostic tests.

**Topic 4: Financial Penalties for PA Paperwork Delays**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify (additional requirement)
- **Rationale:** PA paperwork delays beyond the control of patients, providers, and labs result in unexpected financial penalties.
- **Specific request:** Ban financial penalties (surprise bills) for patients, doctors, and labs resulting from insurance paperwork delays outside their control.

---

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **Lab/DMEPOS RFI (S82 area):** All four points respond to the RFI regarding prior authorization for laboratory tests; advocate for timing-based denial prohibition, redundant procedure elimination, expedited oncology review pathways, and surprise bill protections related to PA delays.

---

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Prohibit insurer denials of laboratory tests solely because PA was pending on the date the specimen was collected.
2. Prohibit requiring patients to repeat procedures when a valid specimen already exists.
3. Establish mandatory expedited PA review pathways for oncology and other time-sensitive diagnostic tests.
4. Prohibit financial penalties (surprise bills) on patients, providers, and labs due to PA paperwork delays beyond their control.
