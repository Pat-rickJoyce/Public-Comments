ORIGINAL COMMENT (COMPLETE)
------------------------
On the SBE gap, you wrote:

"We believe that it is appropriate at this time to continue to exclude issuers on the State-based Exchanges (SBEs), including SBE-FPs, as impacted payers...As we assess the effectiveness of interoperability policies, we will consider whether future rulemaking to include those types of issuers as impacted payers is appropriate."

CMS needs to pick a lane here. If you have the authority to extend these requirements to state-based exchanges, extend them. If you don't, say so explicitly and tell Congress what statutory change is needed to close the gap. What we've gotten instead is three consecutive rulemakings where CMS acknowledges the coverage gap, "encourages" SBEs to consider adopting similar requirements voluntarily, solicits comment on whether to maybe do something about it next time, and moves on to the next section of the preamble.

Here are the levers CMS could pull:

For SBE-FPs (3 states) - These exchanges use HealthCare.gov for enrollment but handle their own QHP certification. CMS could condition continued access to the Federal platform (the Data Services Hub, eligibility services) on meeting interoperability requirements. The QHP Issuer Agreement that SBE-FP issuers sign with CMS already memorializes duties and obligations. Adding interoperability attestation to that agreement would be straightforward.

For fully independent SBEs (21 states): Undoubtedly harder, as CMS doesn't certify those QHPs. But you have a few options:
- ACA Section 1311 essential health benefits and certification standards give the Secretary authority to set minimum QHP certification criteria. CMS could argue interoperability requirements are a condition of QHP status, period, regardless of which exchange certifies the plan.
- Condition federal financial assistance (premium tax credits, CSR payments) on issuer compliance with interoperability requirements. If your plan doesn't support Patient Access API, your enrollees don't get subsidies.

The ~7 million Americans enrolled through SBEs deserve the same interoperability protections as everyone else. Stop teasing us. Carpe that diem and just do it already.
