## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The attachment proposes a working group to define standardized identifiers for health insurance networks and associated plans. Although it does not directly address drug prior authorization, it supports broader CMS interoperability and transparency goals by seeking consistent identifiers that link TIC rates, plan design data, coverage rules, provider directories, and consumer shopping tools.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Working Group for Network and Plan Identifiers**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Fragmented, ad hoc identifiers impede price transparency, health plan shopping, provider directory use, and consumer use of coverage information.
- **Specific request:** Convene a working group to define an identification scheme for networks and associated plans.

**Topic 2: Identifier Requirements and Structure**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify / Modify
- **Rationale:** A useful identifier must resolve the insurer or network management entity, map plans to networks, represent negotiated rates and relevant contractual terms, reduce duplication, and support customized employer-plan networks.
- **Specific request:** Model the identifier on segmented structures like VINs and credit card PANs, with insurer and network components and support for base-network-plus-delta customization.

**Topic 3: Alignment with Interoperability Assets**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED; GENERAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** A public insurer identity would be useful for FHIR Brands, the National Provider Directory, FHIR Provider Directory APIs, and other consumer information exchange use cases.
- **Specific request:** Ensure the network identifier can support provider directory websites/APIs and other interoperability tools.

**Topic 4: Stakeholder Participation and Standards Process**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Identifier rules must reflect insurer operations, TIC production, data-user needs, consumer perspectives, and regulatory oversight.
- **Specific request:** Include insurers, provider directory teams/vendors, TIC file teams/vendors, TIC data users, researchers, patient/consumer organizations, CMS, state regulators, and other interoperability stakeholders.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

No CMS-0062-P numbered solicitation is directly answered. The attachment is most relevant to general RFI solicitations (S66-S82) related to interoperability, public data infrastructure, and consumer-facing transparency, and indirectly to FHIR-IG-REQUIRED topics concerning standards development and adoption.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Establish a working group to define a standard identifier for networks and associated plans.
2. Create a public insurer identifier segment and subordinate network identifier segment.
3. Map health plans unambiguously to the networks they use.
4. Support representation of negotiated rates, contractual terms, and plan coverage rules.
5. Minimize duplicated rate representation in TIC files.
6. Include base-network and delta structures for customized employer plans.
7. Include insurers, vendors, TIC data users, researchers, consumer groups, CMS, and state regulators in the working group.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** No single clear insurer identifier; inconsistent definitions of products, networks, and plans; unclear year-to-year network identity; misalignment between plan years and network identity.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Ambiguity and duplication in price transparency data; reduced utility for provider directories, health plan shopping, and consumer-facing coverage information.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Complex network structures, shared networks, reciprocity arrangements, non-insurer network managers, employer customizations, and plan terms that do not align with calendar years.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Examples provided:** HIOS issuer ID misalignment; different definitions of networks as contract sets versus final provider lists; QHP JSON plan-year limitations; base networks and deltas for employer customizations.
