## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

InterSystems strongly supports the proposed rule's direction toward standards-based electronic prior authorization for drugs and the broader FHIR API framework. The company offers a technically sophisticated 16-page response organized around five implementation principles: mature IGs with predictable versioning, CDex as essential clinical data exchange infrastructure, a centralized endpoint directory, standardized metrics reporting, and workflow-native PA processes that eliminate portal dependency. InterSystems recommends requiring CDex now for clinical documentation, supports the proposed HIPAA shift toward FHIR, urges revisiting the direct data entry exception, and requests standardized metric definitions across all reporting requirements.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Required IGs — Maturity, Versioning, and Conformance**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Support with Modify
- **Rationale:** Required IGs are essential because FHIR alone does not guarantee interoperability. CMS must clarify which IG versions are permissible during transition periods, avoid confusing CMS "expired" terminology with HL7 maintenance status, and explicitly preserve the relationship between base FHIR, US Core, SMART App Launch, Bulk FHIR, and the IGs.
- **Specific request:** Require unexpired standards with controlled versioning and migration periods; clarify compliant versions before and after January 1, 2028; resolve CMS "expiration" vs. HL7 maintenance terminology confusion; clarify treatment of dependent IGs (e.g., HRex) for conformance purposes.

**Topic 2: CDex as Required IG**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Support (require now with graduated path)
- **Rationale:** CDex is sufficiently mature and directly aligned with payer-provider workflows requiring timely clinical information. Without a consistent electronic pathway for clinical documentation exchange, the industry will continue relying on portals, faxes, and manual chart retrieval.
- **Specific request:** Require CDex where clinical documentation is needed for prior authorization now; establish graduated path toward broader CDex use (query- and task-based workflows) for quality measurement, care management, risk adjustment, and payment integrity.

**Topic 3: ATR IG**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Oppose (mandate now)
- **Rationale:** ATR has not demonstrated the maturity or broad real-world production use of other required IGs; attribution models vary significantly across programs.
- **Specific request:** Continue to monitor; allow additional implementation and testing before requiring.

**Topic 4: FAST Security IG**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Support (advance, not yet require)
- **Rationale:** FAST Security and UDAP are valuable for network-based exchange but broad implementation is still emerging.
- **Specific request:** Continue promoting; encourage testing; preserve flexibility for point-to-point exchange during transition; establish clear path to future mandatory use.

**Topic 5: PA for Medical Benefit Drugs**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Medical benefit drugs require clinical context that exists in provider systems but cannot be effectively shared through portals and faxes. Received clinical data should be incorporated into the member's longitudinal record.
- **Specific request:** Require CRD, DTR, and PAS for EHR-workflow PA; ensure clinical data can be incorporated into longitudinal records; support reuse of previously exchanged data.

**Topic 6: PA Metrics — Non-Drug**
- **Provision ID:** API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Position:** Support with Modify
- **Rationale:** Metrics require standardized definitions, denominators, and formats. Should capture cases where standards-based workflows avoid formal PA submission to measure burden reduction.
- **Specific request:** Define standardized metrics; require machine-readable reporting with data dictionaries; capture API-enabled vs. non-API-enabled PA workflows.

**Topic 7: PA Metrics — Drug**
- **Provision ID:** API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Position:** Support with Modify
- **Rationale:** Drug PA metrics must be consistent across states, benefit categories, delegated PBMs, and UM vendors; separate visibility into pharmacy and medical benefit workflows needed.
- **Specific request:** Standardize definitions; stratify by submission method; require PBM/UM vendor support; delay 2028 reporting if specifications unavailable.

**Topic 8: API Endpoint Reporting**
- **Provision ID:** API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Discoverability is a prerequisite for interoperability; centralized registry eliminates fragmented payer-specific directories.
- **Specific request:** Require reporting of URLs, documentation, capability statements, supported IG versions, authentication, and contacts; maintain ongoing centralized registry with historical data beyond 60 days; consider including provider endpoints.

**Topic 9: Formulary Removal from Provider Access and Payer-to-Payer APIs**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED (cross-cutting)
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Formulary is plan-specific, not patient-specific; requiring it in patient-centric APIs creates duplication and confusion.

**Topic 10: HIPAA PA Standards — FHIR Adoption**
- **Provision ID:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Prior authorization requires structured clinical data and workflow integration beyond what administrative transactions alone can achieve; X12 should continue where effective.
- **Specific request:** Recognize CRD, DTR, PAS, and CDex as core HIPAA PA pattern; align administrative and clinical exchange requirements.

**Topic 11: Direct Data Entry Exception**
- **Provision ID:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS
- **Position:** Oppose (current form); Support (revisiting/narrowing)
- **Rationale:** Portal-based workflows trap data outside longitudinal records, undermine automation and auditability, and are the primary source of administrative fragmentation.
- **Specific request:** Revisit and narrow DDE exception; require workflow-native PA processing; clarify exception should not preserve portals when standards-based exchange is available.

**Topic 12: Event Notifications (RFI)**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support (FHIR as complement, not replacement)
- **Rationale:** HL7 v2 ADT is mature and widely deployed; FHIR adds value for cross-organization exchange and richer clinical context.
- **Specific request:** Preserve HL7 v2 ADT; encourage FHIR where it adds value; avoid fragmented state-by-state rules.

**Topic 13: Step Therapy (RFI)**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Step therapy is a strong use case for longitudinal clinical data sharing; Payer-to-Payer API should carry prior therapy history and prior payer determinations.
- **Specific request:** Standardize step therapy criteria through APIs; define conditions for honoring prior determinations; preserve patient visibility into step therapy decision history.

**Topic 14: Lab Tests and DMEPOS (RFI)**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support with recommendations
- **Rationale:** PA requirements should be surfaced before or at ordering time using clinical data already in the patient record.
- **Specific request:** Clarify operationalization under Prior Authorization API; advance standards-based clinical documentation exchange; address delegated vendor role.

**Topic 15: State-Based Exchanges and Severability**
- **Provision ID:** FF-SHOP-REQUIREMENTS, STATE-EXTENSION-PROCESS, GENERAL
- **Position:** Support (extend to SBEs) / Support severability
- **Rationale:** Interoperability requires a national baseline; patients move across state lines and coverage types.
- **Specific request:** Extend to SBEs; coordinate early with states; establish minimum national baseline; discourage open-ended state exceptions; preserve severability for endpoint reporting, required IGs, security, and PA timeframes.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Require unexpired IGs with controlled versioning, migration periods, and testable conformance expectations.
2. Clarify permissible IG versions during transition; resolve CMS "expiration" vs. HL7 maintenance terminology.
3. Explicitly preserve the relationship between base FHIR, US Core, SMART App Launch, Bulk FHIR, and required IGs.
4. Require CDex where clinical documentation is needed for PA now; establish graduated path toward broader CDex adoption.
5. Do not require ATR IG at this time; allow additional implementation and testing.
6. Continue advancing FAST Security; establish clear path toward future required use.
7. Require CRD, DTR, and PAS for medical benefit drug PA within EHR workflow.
8. Ensure received clinical PA data can be incorporated into member longitudinal records.
9. Define standardized PA metrics including common numerators, denominators, reporting periods, decision categories, and calculation methods for both drug and non-drug PA.
10. Require machine-readable metrics reporting with data dictionaries and standardized submission formats.
11. Stratify drug PA metrics by submission method; require PBM/UM vendor support for payer reporting.
12. Maintain centralized national endpoint registry with human-readable and machine-readable formats and historical data beyond 60 days.
13. Consider including or aligning provider endpoint information in the centralized directory.
14. Recognize CRD, DTR, PAS, and CDex as core HIPAA PA workflow standard; preserve X12 where effective.
15. Revisit and narrow the HIPAA direct-data-entry exception; require workflow-native PA processing.
16. Preserve HL7 v2 ADT for event notifications; advance FHIR as complementary pathway.
17. Require strong authentication, encryption, audit logging, and trust frameworks for API-based exchange.
18. Support national test harnesses, reference implementations, and real-world multi-party testing.
19. Use Payer-to-Payer API to carry step therapy history and prior payer determinations.
20. Clarify lab and DMEPOS operationalization under Prior Authorization API; surface requirements early in ordering workflow.
21. Extend interoperability requirements to state-based exchanges; establish minimum national baseline.
22. Support severability to preserve standards-based path if individual provisions are delayed or invalidated.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** IG versioning clarity gap; CDex not yet required despite being sufficiently mature; portal-based workflows preserved by DDE exception; ATR not ready; metric standardization gaps; 60-day endpoint reporting window may be too short.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Without CDex, clinical data exchange remains on portals and faxes; without standardized metrics, public reporting could mislead; inconsistent state-by-state rules would fragment interoperability progress.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** IG version transition complexity; FAST Security trust community infrastructure still emerging; ATR operationally complex; portal ecosystems create locked-in workflows.

- **Affected stakeholders:** Health IT vendors (directly); payers (all types); providers and EHR systems; patients; CMS and state regulators; SDOs; delegated PBMs and UM vendors; laboratories and DMEPOS suppliers.
- **Economic implications:** Standardized endpoint registry and CDex requirement would reduce per-payer/per-vendor integration costs; narrowing DDE exception eliminates costly portal workflows; standardized metrics reduce state-by-state variation; delegated PBM/UM vendor reporting requirements have compliance costs.
- **Technical feasibility:** CDex, CRD/DTR/PAS argued to be sufficiently mature; versioning transitions need careful management; FAST Security and ATR need more production time; real-world multi-party testing essential precondition.

Executive Summary
InterSystems Corporation appreciates the opportunity to comment on the Center for Medicare &
Medicaid Services (CMS) proposed rule, Interoperability Standards and Prior Authorization for
Drugs [CMS-0062-P]. InterSystems supports CMS's efforts to reduce administrative burden,
advance standards-based interoperability, and help patients receive needed care and medications
without unnecessary delay.

InterSystems strongly supports extending standardized electronic prior authorization workflows to
drugs. Patients should not experience delays because clinical information needed for a coverage
decision is trapped in disconnected systems, portals, faxes, or manual documentation workflows.
Drug prior authorization often depends on information already available in provider systems,
including diagnoses, medication history, allergies, laboratory values, prior therapies,
contraindications, procedures, and care-team documentation. Standards-based exchange can help
make this information available at the right time, in the right workflow, and in a form that supports
timely decisions...
