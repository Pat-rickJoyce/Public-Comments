## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** West Virginia Medicaid (State Medicaid FFS Program) — author not individually named
- **Stakeholder type:** Government (State Medicaid Agency / FFS program)
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Direct operational experience implementing Smart Prior Authorizations (iPA) in West Virginia Medicaid; quantified knowledge of state drug PA workload (400+ drugs requiring authorization)

---

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

West Virginia Medicaid FFS opposes the October 2027 implementation timeline as operationally impossible, providing detailed hour-by-hour workload calculations showing that 14,000+ implementation hours are required for 400+ drugs — more than can be completed even working around the clock before the deadline. The state requests additional time to allow sequential rather than simultaneous execution of the NCPDP D.0-to-F6 upgrade and the CMS-0062-P ePA implementation, and additional funding to cover unforeseen vendor and staffing costs.

---

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY (NCPDP standards), PA-DRUG-MEDICAL (FHIR), STATE-EXTENSION-PROCESS, IMPLEMENTATION-TIMELINE
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** CMS-0062-P (proposed rule reference only); NCPDP D.0 to F6 transition (August 14, 2027 start, April 14, 2028 full compliance)
- **Standards referenced:** FHIR (generic reference via vendor), NCPDP D.0, NCPDP F6 standard

---

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Implementation Timeline**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY, PA-DRUG-MEDICAL, STATE-EXTENSION-PROCESS
- **Position:** Oppose (current date); request extension
- **Rationale:** WV has 400+ drugs requiring PA. Creating and testing ePA question sets requires ~35 hours per drug (10 hours rule set development + 25 hours system entry and testing). Total: ~14,000 hours = 583 continuous days at full capacity, which exceeds the October 2027 deadline even if working 24/7. Vendor has confirmed the timeline is not realistic. Prior implementation of Smart Prior Authorizations (iPA) took nearly 2 years.
- **Specific request:** Grant a later implementation date that allows the NCPDP D.0-to-F6 upgrade to complete before beginning ePA implementation.

**Topic 2: NCPDP Standard Transition Overlap**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Modify (sequencing)
- **Rationale:** The NCPDP D.0-to-F6 transition was announced before CMS-0062-P and runs from August 2027 through April 2028 — directly overlapping with the October 2027 ePA compliance date. Attempting both upgrades simultaneously would cause confusion for the claims vendor, state Medicaid program, and enrolled providers.
- **Specific request:** Allow the NCPDP upgrade to complete before implementing ePA requirements; sequence the two projects rather than running them in parallel.

**Topic 3: Cost and Budget**
- **Provision ID:** COST-RESOURCES / STATE-EXTENSION-PROCESS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** State Medicaid programs operate with constrained budgets; additional vendor procurement, staff overtime, system upgrades, and unforeseen costs were not included in the budget proposal. Legislative appropriation would be required.
- **Specific request:** Allow additional time for finance planning and legislative appropriations; CMS should recognize that state Medicaid programs require budget cycle lead time for projects of this magnitude.

---

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S19:** Directly addresses state Medicaid FFS readiness for NCPDP-based pharmacy ePA; WV states readiness is not achievable by October 2027 given NCPDP D.0-to-F6 overlap and internal workload constraints.
- **S21:** Addresses whether October 2027 timeline is achievable; WV quantifies that it is mathematically not achievable even with maximum staffing, and that the vendor has confirmed the same.
- **S36/S38:** Implicitly supports the extension process and requests its use; advocates for sufficient extension duration to allow sequential completion of NCPDP F6 transition before ePA implementation.
- **S22:** Raises cost concerns for NCPDP implementation; WV needs additional budget planning and legislative appropriations.

---

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Grant a later implementation date — at minimum, after the NCPDP D.0-to-F6 transition is complete (i.e., after April 14, 2028).
2. Allow the NCPDP standard upgrade to occur before requiring ePA implementation to avoid simultaneous system upgrades.
3. Allow additional time for state finance planning and legislative budget allocation.
4. Recognize that state Medicaid programs do not have discretionary funds for unplanned vendor and staffing costs; federal support or lead time is needed.

---

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Implementation timeline is mathematically impossible given workload; simultaneous NCPDP D.0-to-F6 upgrade and ePA implementation will cause systemic confusion; state Medicaid budget constraints make unplanned procurement impossible without legislative action.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Simultaneous upgrades described as "a disaster" that would cause "a great deal of issues and complications" for the claims vendor, state program, and enrolled providers.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** 14,000+ hours of work required for 400+ drug question sets; vendor confirmed October 2027 is not achievable and would require additional hiring; NCPDP D.0-to-F6 transition overlap; no budgetary provision for additional vendor; legislative appropriation required for staffing and system costs.
