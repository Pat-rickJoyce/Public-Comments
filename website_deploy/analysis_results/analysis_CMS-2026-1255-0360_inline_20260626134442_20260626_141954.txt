ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
I am writing to submit my strong support for this proposed rule, which takes critical steps toward modernizing healthcare data exchange and lowering administrative barriers for both patients and providers. First, I want to applaud the Trump Administration for its continued leadership on this front. This administration has done more than any past presidency to aggressively advance the interoperability of electronic health records (EHRs). By treating healthcare data silos as a barrier to competitive markets and patient freedom, this administration is fundamentally reshaping how healthcare operates in America. Extending these interoperability standards to prescription drug prior authorizations is a natural and highly necessary continuation of that legacy. These technical advancements will have a massive, direct benefit on patients and the overall cost of care. For too long, patients have suffered from delayed treatments, duplicate testing, and administrative bottlenecks because different health IT systems refuse to speak to one another. Embracing FHIR standards and streamlining the prior authorization process will allow true care to happen seamlessly...
