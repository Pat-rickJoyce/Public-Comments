**1. COMMENTER INFORMATION**
- Author/Organization name: Helsesoft LLC / GreenLight PA
- Stakeholder type: Technology Vendor
- Credentials or expertise mentioned: Washington State health technology company; developer of GreenLight PA prior authorization intelligence platform for dermatology; maintains structured library of PA criteria for dermatology biologics across 200+ drug-insurer-state combinations; founded by physician

**2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
Helsesoft LLC strongly supports CMS-0062-P's extension of electronic prior authorization requirements to drugs. The company urges CMS to make Da Vinci CRD and DTR implementation guides mandatory, require specialty-level granularity in public PA metrics, and address the systemic divergence between payers' published PA criteria and their actual approval behavior.

**3. REGULATORY CONTEXT**
- Specific sections/provisions addressed: CMS-0062-P; builds on CMS-0057-F; Sections II.B, II.C, II.C.7, II.E; RFI III.D (Step Therapy); RFI III.C (Payer API Implementation)
- Regulatory citations mentioned: 91 FR 19890; MA, Medicaid, CHIP, QHP programs
- Standards referenced: FHIR, NCPDP Formulary and Benefit Standard, Da Vinci CRD, DTR, PAS

**KEY RECOMMENDATIONS**
1. Make Da Vinci CRD and DTR mandatory (not optional) for Prior Authorization API for drugs
2. Require specialty-level granularity in public PA metrics
3. Require payers to publish step therapy requirements in structured, machine-readable form
4. Include step therapy criterion citation in denial communications
5. Require annual attestation that published step therapy requirements match applied criteria

**PRIMARY CONCERN**
Systemic divergence between payers' published prior authorization criteria and criteria actually applied in approval/denial decisions. Across 200+ drug-insurer-state combinations monitored, published payer criteria do not reliably predict actual approval behavior.

**KEY QUOTE**
"Providers build PA submissions around published criteria that may not reflect what the insurer actually requires, leading to avoidable denials, resubmission cycles, and delays in patient access to therapy."

**THEMES**
- Interoperability (FHIR, NCPDP, Da Vinci standards)
- Transparency/Accountability (published vs. applied criteria divergence)
- Specialty Care (dermatology biologics focus)
- Provider Burden (manual PDF interpretation, administrative complexity)
- Step Therapy (inconsistent enforcement, payer variation)

I. Introduction and Commenter Identity
Helsesoft LLC is a Washington State health technology company and the developer of
GreenLight PA, a prior authorization intelligence platform built specifically for dermatology.
GreenLight PA maintains a structured, machine-readable library of prior authorization (PA)
criteria for dermatology biologics — including agents such as dupilumab, tralokinumab,
risankizumab, and secukinumab — mapped at the drug, insurer, and state level across major
commercial payers including Medicare Advantage organizations, Medicaid managed care plans,
and commercial QHP issuers. The platform is EHR-independent and contains no protected
health information (PHI) by architectural design, enabling deployment across practice settings of
all sizes.
Helsesoft LLC was founded by a physician with direct experience in healthcare workflow design
and clinical operations, and has built GreenLight PA in close collaboration with practicing
dermatologists. The company's development work has required sustained, direct engagement
with payer coverage policy documents across multiple drug-indication combinations, insurers,
and states — giving us ground-level technical understanding of how prior authorization criteria
are published, structured, updated, and applied in practice. This comment reflects that
operational experience...
