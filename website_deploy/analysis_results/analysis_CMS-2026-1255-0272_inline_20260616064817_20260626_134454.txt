ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
I am a registered nurse working in a cardiac cath lab and serve as nursing faculty at a local community college. I support the proposed rule to improve interoperability standards and streamline prior authorization processes for medications.<br/>In clinical practice, I frequently observe delays in obtaining medications due to challenges in communication between healthcare providers, pharmacies, and insurance companies. These delays can occur during hospital discharge when patients require timely access to medications that are critical to their recovery and ongoing treatment.<br/>For example, cardiovascular patients discharged after percutaneous coronary intervention often require antiplatelet therapy. Delays related to prior authorization requirements, incomplete transmission of clinical information, or difficulties communicating supporting documentation can postpone access to these medications. Such delays may increase patient anxiety, create additional workload for healthcare staff, and potentially impact patient outcomes.<br/>I believe improved interoperability between electronic health records, pharmacies, and payers has the potential to reduce administrative burden and improve the efficiency of prior authorization processes. Allowing clinical information to be exchanged electronically in a standardized format could help ensure that necessary documentation is available to support medication approval decisions more quickly.<br/>While I support the intent of this proposal, I encourage CMS to consider the implementation burden on healthcare organizations and to provide adequate support, education, and transition time for healthcare systems adopting new interoperability requirements.<br/><br/>Thank you for the opportunity to comment on this proposed rule.<br/><br/>Rusty Das, MSN, RN, CMSRN<br/>DNP Student<br/>PennWest University...
