## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The commenter argues that prior authorization for time-sensitive drugs and laboratory tests can harm patient care and safety, especially for solid organ transplant recipients. They request exemptions for transplant status and urgent diagnostics or medications such as antivirals, immunosuppression, complement inhibitors, cf-DNA, and monoclonal antibodies.

- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify.
- **Rationale:** Prior authorization delays for transplant-related medications and diagnostic tests can negatively affect patient safety and continuity of care.
