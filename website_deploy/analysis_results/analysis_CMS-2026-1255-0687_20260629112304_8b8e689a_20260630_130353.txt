# Comment Analysis Template: CMS-2026-1255-0001

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Radiopharmaceutical Manufacturers & Theranostics Association (RMTA), a division of the National Electrical Manufacturers Association; signed by Susan K. Bunning, Managing Director.
- **Stakeholder type:** Other - manufacturer/trade association
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Represents radiopharmaceutical and medical imaging manufacturers; describes clinical use of FDA-approved diagnostic radiopharmaceuticals for cancer, cardiac disease, Alzheimer's disease, and Parkinson's disease.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

RMTA supports CMS's proposed electronic prior authorization reforms for drugs and biologics, especially stronger transparency, timeliness, and specific denial reasons. Its central request is that CMS clarify in the final rule that all PA reforms apply equally to FDA-approved radiopharmaceuticals classified as drugs or biologics, and that PA requirements for the radiopharmaceutical be harmonized with the associated imaging procedure.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DENIAL-REASONS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS; MEDICAL-VS-PHARMACY-BENEFIT / GENERAL.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** No CFR sections cited directly.
- **Standards referenced:** Electronic prior authorization transactions generally; no specific FHIR IG or NCPDP standard version is named.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Finalize drug e-PA reforms**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Standardized electronic transactions can reduce administrative burden, minimize delays in therapy, and support informed treatment decisions through timely coverage and utilization-management information.
- **Specific request:** Finalize the proposed rule extending electronic PA protections to FDA-approved drugs and biologics across affected payers.

**Topic 2: Radiopharmaceutical inclusion**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL
- **Position:** Clarify
- **Rationale:** Radiopharmaceuticals are FDA-approved under NDAs, ANDAs, or BLAs, even if CMS sometimes treats them differently for payment because of radioactive decay. RMTA argues those payment distinctions should not affect PA reform coverage.
- **Specific request:** Clarify that PA reforms apply equally to FDA-approved drugs and biologics that CMS classifies as radiopharmaceuticals.

**Topic 3: Denial reason specificity**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS
- **Position:** Support / Clarify
- **Rationale:** Formulaic denials do not help providers understand whether documentation failed to support the drug or whether plan criteria were unmet.
- **Specific request:** Require denial reasons to explain the plan coverage criteria, why documentation was insufficient, or why the drug was not deemed necessary, and what actions are needed to resubmit or appeal.

**Topic 4: Accelerated decision timeframes and oversight**
- **Provision ID:** PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS
- **Position:** Support / Modify
- **Rationale:** Faster timeframes can improve access, but without oversight they could produce more denials or inappropriate utilization management.
- **Specific request:** Pair accelerated PA response times with meaningful CMS monitoring, transparency, accountability, and enforcement resources.

**Topic 5: Harmonization with imaging procedure authorization**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; MEDICAL-VS-PHARMACY-BENEFIT / GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify
- **Rationale:** Diagnostic radiopharmaceuticals are administered with SPECT, PET, or PET/CT procedures, and separate uncoordinated PA processes could undermine timely access.
- **Specific request:** Clarify whether PA for the radiopharmaceutical will be harmonized with PA for the related imaging procedure.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S12:** Supports electronic PA for medical benefit drugs and asks CMS to include radiopharmaceuticals that are FDA-approved as drugs or biologics.
- **S15:** Requests clarification on how radiopharmaceutical drug PA will coordinate with authorization for related imaging procedures, raising a benefit/category boundary issue.
- **S16:** Supports specific denial reasons and asks CMS to require actionable, non-formulaic explanations tied to plan criteria and documentation deficiencies.
- **S20:** Supports maximum response times but warns compressed deadlines must not create increased denials or inappropriate utilization management.
- **S21:** Implied support for implementation if paired with oversight, transparency, accountability, and enforcement resources.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Finalize the proposed electronic PA reforms for drugs and biologics.
2. Clarify that FDA-approved radiopharmaceuticals are covered by the PA reforms even when CMS classifies them differently for payment purposes.
3. Require specific denial reasons that identify plan criteria, documentation gaps, and resubmission or appeal steps.
4. Pair accelerated PA timeframes with CMS oversight, enforcement resources, monitoring, and transparency.
5. Clarify whether radiopharmaceutical PA will be harmonized with PA for SPECT, PET, or PET/CT imaging procedures.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Possible ambiguity about radiopharmaceutical coverage, non-actionable denial reasons, increased denials under compressed timelines, and uncoordinated drug/procedure authorizations.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Delays in access to diagnostic and therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals for patients with cancer, Alzheimer's disease, cardiovascular disease, and other serious conditions.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Distinct CMS payment classification for radiopharmaceuticals and the operational need to coordinate product and imaging procedure authorizations.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** More than 30 published studies reportedly confirm beta amyloid PET leads to changes in diagnosis and treatment management plans.
- **Studies or research referenced:** General reference to CMS-approved and other clinical studies supporting diagnostic radiopharmaceutical utility; no individual study citations are provided.
- **Examples provided:** Beta amyloid PET, oncologic use in advanced breast cancer and neuroendocrine cancer, and administration with SPECT, PET, or PET/CT.

## 9. IMPACT ASSESSMENT

- **Affected stakeholders:** Patients using radiopharmaceuticals, clinicians, imaging providers, MA organizations, Medicaid/CHIP programs, QHPs, radiopharmaceutical manufacturers, and CMS.
- **Economic implications:** Streamlined PA may reduce administrative burden for providers and plans; unclear or duplicative PA could delay use of high-value diagnostic products.
- **Technical feasibility:** RMTA supports standardized electronic transactions but highlights the need for coordinated workflows across product and imaging-procedure authorization.
- **Patient care impact:** Clarifying radiopharmaceutical coverage could improve access for patients with cancer, cognitive impairment, cardiac disease, and other serious conditions.
