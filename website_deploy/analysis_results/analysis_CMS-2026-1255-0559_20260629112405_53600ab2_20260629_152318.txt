# Comment Analysis Template: CMS-2026-1255-0001

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Biodesix, Inc., signed by Robin H. Cowie, Chief Financial Officer.
- **Stakeholder type:** Healthcare Provider; Technology/Diagnostics Vendor; Other clinical laboratory.
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Enrolled Medicare provider of lung cancer diagnostic laboratory tests, including Nodify XL2, Nodify CDT, and VeriStrat; tests are covered under MAC LCDs and/or approved as Advanced Diagnostic Laboratory Tests.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Biodesix responds to CMS's laboratory-test prior authorization RFI and argues that MA organizations misuse prior authorization and prepayment review to undercut coverage for NCD- and LCD-covered laboratory tests. The commenter asks CMS to prohibit PA for NCD/LCD-covered tests or, at minimum, require MAOs to accept test requisition form documentation, allow post-specimen authorization timing, permit laboratories to initiate PA, and create an expedited enforcement pathway.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** GENERAL/RFI on laboratory tests and DMEPOS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS by analogy to PA timing; TRANSPARENCY/enforcement; REGULATORY-AUTHORITY as applied to MAO obligations.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** Social Security Act §1852(a)(1)(A); 42 CFR §422.101(a), §422.101(b)(3), §422.101(c)(1), §422.138(b), §422.566(c)(1)(ii), §410.32(d)(3), §414.510, §414.502; Medicare Managed Care Manual chapter 4 §§90.4.1 and 110.1.1; Medicare Program Integrity Manual chapter 3 §3.2.3.7.
- **Standards referenced:** No FHIR or NCPDP standards are central; comment focuses on Medicare coverage rules, LCDs, NCDs, ADLTs, TRFs, and prior authorization operations.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Prohibit PA for NCD/LCD-Covered Laboratory Tests**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** MAOs must cover original Medicare-covered services and should not use PA to create inappropriate barriers to NCD- or LCD-covered services.
- **Specific request:** Bar MAOs from using prior authorization for items or services covered by an NCD or LCD.

**Topic 2: TRF as Valid Medical Documentation**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify
- **Rationale:** Medicare rules and MAC policies contemplate initial review of laboratory order, diagnostic information, and order-processing documentation, which is commonly available on detailed TRFs.
- **Specific request:** Clarify that MAOs must consider TRF information and approve coverage when the TRF satisfies NCD/LCD criteria unless contradicted elsewhere in the medical record.

**Topic 3: Date of Service and Post-Specimen Authorization**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify/Modify
- **Rationale:** MAOs sometimes deny PA because a specimen was collected before authorization, even though the test was not performed before the PA request.
- **Specific request:** Require acceptance of PA requests within 30 days after the DOS when specimen collection date must be used as DOS.

**Topic 4: Laboratories Must Be Allowed to Request PA**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support/Clarify
- **Rationale:** Laboratories are providers furnishing or intending to furnish services and are better positioned than ordering physicians to complete PA requests for unique diagnostics.
- **Specific request:** Finalize/confirm that MA plans must allow laboratories to initiate PA requests and establish an enforcement pathway.

**Topic 5: Prepayment Review for NCD/LCD-Covered Tests**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Burdensome prepayment review can function like PA and delay or deny payment for medically necessary LCD-covered testing.
- **Specific request:** Prohibit broad prepayment review for NCD/LCD-covered tests or clarify that TRF information responsive to coverage criteria satisfies prepayment review obligations.

**Topic 6: Enforcement Pathway**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL; REGULATORY-AUTHORITY
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Clarifications will not be effective without a practical route to report and correct MAO violations.
- **Specific request:** Establish a clear, accessible, expedited complaint and enforcement pathway.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S66-S82:** Responds to the RFI on laboratory tests, especially questions about laboratory PA impact, TRF validity, specimen timing, and technology gaps.
- **Laboratory PA impact RFI:** States that MAO PA can block access to Medicare-covered diagnostic tests and should be prohibited for NCD/LCD-covered services.
- **TRF RFI:** Argues TRFs should be accepted as part of the medical record when they contain coverage-criteria information.
- **Specimen collection timing RFI:** Requests a rule allowing PA submission up to 30 days after specimen collection where the DOS policy applies.
- **Other issues RFI:** Requests confirmation that laboratories may request PA directly and that MAOs may not use prepayment review to evade coverage obligations.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Prohibit MAO prior authorization for NCD- or LCD-covered tests.
2. Clarify MAOs must consider TRF information as medical record documentation.
3. Require coverage where TRF information satisfies NCD/LCD criteria absent contradictory medical record evidence.
4. Allow PA requests up to 30 days after specimen collection when specimen collection is treated as the DOS.
5. Confirm laboratories may initiate PA requests for tests they furnish.
6. Prohibit or restrict prepayment review for NCD/LCD-covered tests.
7. Establish an accessible, expedited enforcement pathway for complaints.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** MAO misuse of PA, refusal to accept TRF documentation, improper DOS-based denials, restrictions on lab-initiated PA, and burdensome prepayment review.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Delayed testing, administrative burden on laboratories and ordering physicians, patient confusion, unpaid medically necessary tests, and reduced access to lung cancer diagnostics.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** MAO policies inconsistent with MAC coverage and documentation practices; plan refusal to operationalize original Medicare coverage rules.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** One national health plan denied 88% of Biodesix's Medicare-covered Nodify XL2, Nodify CDT, and VeriStrat claims for MA enrollees between 2020 and 2024 due to prepayment review.
- **Studies or research referenced:** Not primarily research-based; relies on Medicare regulations, manuals, MAC LCDs/articles, ADLT status, and operational experience.
- **Examples provided:** Nodify XL2, Nodify CDT, and VeriStrat tests; WPS and Novitas MAC policies; MAO denial of PA after specimen collection.

## 9. IMPACT ASSESSMENT

- **Affected stakeholders:** Medicare Advantage enrollees needing lung cancer diagnostics, ordering physicians, clinical laboratories, MAOs, MACs, and CMS oversight staff.
- **Economic implications:** Inappropriate denials and prepayment review can cause revenue loss for laboratories and add administrative costs for providers.
- **Technical feasibility:** Issue is less about new technology than policy alignment, documentation acceptance, and enforcement.
- **Patient care impact:** Positive if CMS acts; reducing PA misuse would improve timely access to covered diagnostic testing.
