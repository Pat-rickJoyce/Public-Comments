**Topic 1: Denial of GLP-1 drug coverage after repeated prior authorization attempts**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY

- **Rationale:** The commenter reports that prior authorization barriers have prevented access to Ozempic/GLP-1 therapy despite consistent recommendations from multiple specialists and primary care.
