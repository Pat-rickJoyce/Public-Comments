ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
I am nurse practitioner in Florida. Prior authorization (PA) and step therapy requirements create significant barriers to timely patient care. While utilization management can play a role in promoting evidence-based care, the current processes frequently result in treatment delays, interruptions in therapy, increased administrative burden, and patient frustration. Prior authorization requirements often delay medically necessary treatment while patients wait for payer review and approval. Step therapy policies can require patients to fail therapies that may be less effective or inappropriate for their individual clinical circumstances before accessing the treatment recommended by their clinician.<br/>These challenges are particularly burdensome for physician-administered therapies, where delays can postpone treatment initiation and worsen patient outcomes.
