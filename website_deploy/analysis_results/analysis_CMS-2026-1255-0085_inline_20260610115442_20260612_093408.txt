## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The commenter recommends that CGRP inhibitor medications be treated as first-line migraine therapy consistent with the cited American Headache Society position statement. They also ask CMS to prevent forced medication switching within a drug class, citing a personal example where switching among CGRP inhibitors took nine months and harmed quality of life.

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS; GENERAL

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: CGRP inhibitors as first-line migraine therapy**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** The commenter states that AHS guidance supports CGRP inhibitors as first-line therapy for migraine.
- **Specific request:** Add or recognize CGRP inhibitor medications as first-line migraine treatment options in coverage criteria.

**Topic 2: Forced switching within a medication class**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Forced cycling through therapeutically similar drugs can delay return to an effective medication and harm patient wellbeing.
- **Specific request:** Prevent forced switching of medications within the CGRP inhibitor class.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S16:** The comment indirectly addresses denial criteria by opposing coverage rules that force switching or cycling through CGRP inhibitors despite a known effective medication.
- **S20:** The nine-month forced-switching example addresses delays in access to effective therapy.
- **S66-S82:** The comment is a general recommendation on step therapy, medication switching, and migraine guideline alignment.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Recognize CGRP inhibitors as first-line options for migraine therapy.
2. Align payer criteria with AHS guidance on CGRP inhibitor use.
3. Prevent forced switching or cycling through drugs within the same therapeutic class when a patient has an effective medication.

ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
Hello, my name is Ashley Bailey and I am a 4th year neurology resident at Barrow Neurological Institute. I see headache patients in my clinic and I would like to promote adding CGRP inhibitor medications as a first line option for migraine therapy as indicated by the AHS position statement from 2025 recommending its use as a first line option. <br/><br/>I would also like to promote the prevention of forced switching of medications within a class. A personal example of this is my mother. She was required to cycle through 3 CGRP inhibitors before she could switch back to galcanezumab which was her original medication. This took 9 months to undertake and that year of her life was significantly detrimentally affected.
