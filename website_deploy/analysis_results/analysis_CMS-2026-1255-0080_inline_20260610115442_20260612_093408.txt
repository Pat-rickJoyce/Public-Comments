ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
Hello, I am a friend to an individual who is debilitated by migraine.  She has been impacted with migraine throughout her life which has caused disruption in her career and her family life.  I have watched her try many medications and been denied access to medications that her physician has prescribed.  I would ask CMS to limit repeated fail first requirements and allow physicians to prescribe the most appropriate treatment for their patient.  Better access to targeted therapies would get patients the treatments they deserve so they can be present in their life and not miss work and important family events.  Thank you for your consideration.
