## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Need for Standardized Network and Plan Identifiers**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Network and plan identification are necessary for accurate price transparency data, healthcare shopping, and exchange of product information across stakeholders.
- **Specific request:** Establish a standardized network identifier rather than relying on fragmented or insurer-specific identifiers.

**Topic 2: Standards Development Pathway**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED; GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** A formal SDO process may take years, but a voluntary industry-led standard could be developed faster and later formalized. CMS can recognize external standards or de facto technical artifacts outside detailed regulation.
- **Specific request:** Use an industry-led working group and, when stable, CMS recognition through external standards references or published schemas/artifacts.

**Topic 3: Conformance and Oversight**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Voluntary collaboration can accelerate early adoption, but fragmentation may persist without regulatory oversight, as shown by the analogy to VIN assignment rules.
- **Specific request:** Consider both voluntary adoption mechanisms and formal oversight to ensure identifiers are unique, timely, accurate, and conformant.

**Topic 4: Identifier Design for Complex Insurance Products**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify
- **Rationale:** Insurers define networks inconsistently, corporate entities do not always match consumer-facing brands, and ERISA/employer products often lack clear product identity.
- **Specific request:** Develop guidance defining insurers, networks, product identity, base networks, and deltas for customized products.
