ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
My father passed away from stage 4 stomach cancer. He was diagnosed January of 2024 and passed away January 2025. His symptoms were dismissed and no early screening test were available for early detection. His treatment was often delayed due to insurance approval requirements and denials from insurance for certain treatments and medication. As patients and family it is frustrating and unacceptable that insurances get to decide when or if treatment is needed instead of our doctors. Stomach cancer is not a size fits all and bio magnet testing is so so important in choosing the correct and most effective treatments. This issue needs to be addressed and resolved immediately.
