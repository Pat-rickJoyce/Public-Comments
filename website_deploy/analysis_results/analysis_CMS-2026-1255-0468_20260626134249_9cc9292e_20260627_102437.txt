- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Operates the largest connected live consumer health data network in the market; holds data on 11 million people (24M encounters, 75M claims, 42M prescriptions); CMS-Aligned Network with live TEFCA integration; CARIN Alliance member; works with hundreds of payers operationally.

---

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

b.well Connected Health strongly supports CMS-0062-P across all major provisions — required FHIR IGs, centralized endpoint directory, FHIR drug PA, FF-SHOP extension, and the HIPAA X12N 278 replacement — and offers five targeted recommendations to maximize patient-visible benefit. Their core ask is to extend the rule's structural gains directly to patients: consumer-facing real-time prescription benefit checks, formulary data in FHIR, PA status in the Patient Access API, and a pathway toward real-world API performance enforcement beyond conformance testing. The submission is coordinated with the CARIN Alliance and RxUtility filings.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: HIPAA PA Standards — Align Compliance Dates and Add Patient Access**
- **Provision ID:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS
- **Position:** Support / Modify
- **Rationale:** HIPAA provision is the broadest reach of the rule (full covered-entity universe). Current SMART App Launch v2.0.0 citation predates PKCE; 24/36-month HIPAA windows don't align with Oct 1, 2027 API dates, creating parallel X12/FHIR maintenance burden.
- **Specific request:** (a) Update SMART App Launch citation to v2.2 with PKCE; (b) align HIPAA compliance windows with October 1, 2027 API dates; (c) include preamble reconciliation guidance on Da Vinci CDex vs. finalized X12N 275 attachment standard; (d) set timeline for HIPAA-covered entities to expose data through Patient Access API.

**Topic 2: Endpoint Discovery — National Directory as Primary Pathway**
- **Provision ID:** API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING
- **Position:** Support / Modify
- **Rationale:** Current payer endpoint publishing is fragmented (developer portals, bilateral agreements); patients cannot reliably connect apps to new plans when changing coverage.
- **Specific request:** Finalize FAST National Directory IG as primary endpoint-discovery pathway; use bilateral §422.119(h) reporting as transition mechanism; require directory to be FHIR-native, freely available, expose OAuth/SMART metadata, support FHIR Subscriptions, and carry a published SLA.

**Topic 3: Consumer-Facing RTPB — Extend to Patients by October 2027**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Modify (expand scope)
- **Rationale:** ~1/3 of approved prescriptions never picked up due to cost surprise at pharmacy counter. CARIN Alliance consumer RTPBC FHIR IG published since 2020; NCPDP Foundation grant in June 2025 harmonizing consumer IG with provider-facing RTPB standard.
- **Specific request:** Require impacted payers to expose consumer-facing RTPBC endpoint via Patient Access API by October 1, 2027 including patient out-of-pocket costs, formulary tier, PA flag, lower-cost alternatives, manufacturer coupons, and cash prices alongside insurance-priced cost.

**Topic 4: Reference DTR Questionnaires — Accelerate Medical-Benefit Drug PA Convergence**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL
- **Position:** Support / Modify
- **Rationale:** October 1, 2027 compliance date is achievable, but payer-EHR bilateral negotiation of DTR Questionnaire shapes for each drug category creates fragmentation. Several major biologics exist in both Part B and Part D forms, creating benefit-pathway confusion at prescribing.
- **Specific request:** CMS commit in Final Rule, in coordination with HL7 and Da Vinci Project, to publish reference DTR Questionnaires for top 20 medical-benefit drug PA categories within 6 months of Final Rule; name DTR explicitly as the medical-benefit parallel to RTPB.

**Topic 5: Real-World API Usability — Signal Longer-Term Direction**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED / API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Position:** Support / Modify
- **Rationale:** Conformance testing (e.g., HL7 Inferno) validates API presence but not real-world performance. No consistent test methods or pass criteria exist yet across multiple public harnesses.
- **Specific request:** Acknowledge real-world API usability as goal; commit CMS/ONC to multi-stakeholder process (HL7/Da Vinci/CARIN) over 2 years; encourage voluntary use of existing harnesses now; implement open third-party access with security-based rate limits (no commercial gatekeeping) immediately in final rule.

**Topic 6: FHIR IG Version Specificity**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Without named versions, "required" can mean different things at different payers.
- **Specific request:** Name specific STU versions in final rule: CARIN BB 2.2.0, Da Vinci PDex 2.1.0, CRD 2.2.1, DTR 2.2.0, PAS 2.2.1, current Bulk Data Access, current CDex; cap concurrent live versions at two; require 12-month overlap before sunset; require payers to publish version metadata.

**Topic 7: Formulary Data Dual Exposure**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY / FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Consumer apps and AI assistants shouldn't need to translate between NCPDP and FHIR for the same underlying formulary data.
- **Specific request:** Require impacted payers to expose formulary data through both NCPDP F&B (per proposal) and FHIR PDex US Drug Formulary IG; require daily refresh for formulary changes affecting cost-share or PA status; require machine-usable formulary data.

**Topic 8: FF-SHOP Requirements**
- **Provision ID:** FF-SHOP-REQUIREMENTS
- **Position:** Support / Clarify
- **Rationale:** Many issuers already operate in both individual and small-group markets with the same FHIR infrastructure.
- **Specific request:** Explicitly permit FF-SHOP issuers to use existing individual-market FHIR infrastructure; align FF-SHOP compliance date with FF-Exchange compliance date.

**Topic 9: Open Payments CMP**
- **Provision ID:** OPEN-PAYMENTS-CMP
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Supports the "failure to report" definition as a transparency-protective measure.
- **Specific request:** No modification requested.

**Topic 10: RFI Responses — Event Notifications, Resiliency, API Implementation, Step Therapy, Lab/DMEPOS**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support / Modify
- **Rationale:** Detailed operational experience informs specific technical recommendations across RFI topics.
- **Specific request:** Numerous specific recommendations (see Solicitation Responses and Recommendations sections).

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Update SMART App Launch citation from v2.0.0 to v2.2 with PKCE as the security baseline.
2. Align HIPAA compliance windows (24/36 months) with October 1, 2027 API compliance dates to enable single migration.
3. Include preamble reconciliation guidance for Da Vinci CDex vs. X12N 275 attachment standard coexistence.
4. Require covered entities under HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS to expose data through Patient Access API with defined timeline.
5. Finalize FAST National Directory IG as primary endpoint-discovery pathway; use bilateral §422.119(h) reporting as transition mechanism.
6. Require consumer-facing RTPBC endpoint via Patient Access API by October 1, 2027, including out-of-pocket costs, formulary tier, PA flag, alternatives, and cash prices.
7. Require daily formulary refresh; constrained NCPDP coverage restriction value set; RxNorm therapeutic alternatives; access on consistent terms for HIPAA-compliant apps.
8. Require impacted payers to expose formulary data through FHIR PDex US Drug Formulary IG in addition to NCPDP F&B.
9. CMS commit in Final Rule to publish reference DTR Questionnaires for top 20 medical-benefit drug PA categories within 6 months of Final Rule.
10. Name DTR explicitly as medical-benefit PA parallel to RTPB on the same October 1, 2027 timeline.
11. Acknowledge real-world API usability as goal; commit CMS/ONC to multi-stakeholder process (HL7/Da Vinci/CARIN) over 2 years for test methods.
12. Implement open third-party API access with security-based rate limits and no commercial-agreement gatekeeping in final rule.
13. Name specific STU versions of all required IGs in final rule (CRD 2.2.1, DTR 2.2.0, PAS 2.2.1, CARIN BB 2.2.0, PDex 2.1.0, current Bulk Data, CDex).
14. Cap concurrent live versions of any required standard at two; require 12-month overlap before sunset; require 90-day advance notice.
15. Explicitly sunset fax/phone PA channels for in-scope pharmacy-benefit drugs no later than 6 months after compliance date.
16. Define minimum API documentation set: CapabilityStatement URL, SearchParameters, conformance test results, sandbox URL, technical contact.
17. Add quality API metrics: p95/p99 latency, error rates, OAuth success rate, unique active clients; publish in FHIR Measure.
18. Cap state Medicaid/CHIP extensions at two consecutive years; require quarterly public progress reporting; decouple from HIPAA finalization.
19. Bind PA denial reason to coded value set (HL7 v3 ActReason + X12 Claim Adjustment Reason Codes); permit supplementary free text.
20. Publish SLA-clock-pause guidance for DTR follow-up question loops; clarify clock runs in calendar hours from receipt of sufficient documentation.
21. Adopt HL7 FHIR Subscriptions Framework and Da Vinci Unsolicited Notifications IG for event notifications; expand recipient set; require structured content.
22. Require TLS 1.3 and OAuth 2.1 with PKCE across all required APIs; encourage Health-ISAC participation; 72-hour API incident disclosure to CMS.
23. Require Payer-to-Payer API to transmit structured step-therapy history; require receiving payers to credit prior step-therapy determinations with 5-year look-back default.
24. Extend FHIR CRD/DTR/PAS pipeline to laboratory tests and DMEPOS; publish reference DTR Questionnaires for high-volume lab and DMEPOS categories.
25. Include language-access requirements in reference DTR Questionnaires (FHIR Questionnaire with language-tagged display strings).

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Conformance testing is not the same as real-world API performance; current test methods are not comparable across harnesses and could enable cherry-picking. HIPAA compliance timeline misalignment will force parallel X12/FHIR maintenance. Endpoint discovery fragmentation prevents patients from connecting apps to new plans. Cost surprise at pharmacy counter (1/3 of approved prescriptions never picked up) not fully addressed by provider-facing RTPB alone.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Without consumer-facing RTPBC, patients won't benefit from prescription benefit transparency the rule creates. Without named IG versions, "required" becomes ambiguous. Without real-world API usability framework, conformance certificates won't guarantee functioning APIs in production.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Payer FHIR implementations vary in ways that affect app behavior when patients change plans; bilateral DTR Questionnaire shape negotiation creates fragmentation; endpoint discovery behind developer-portal click-throughs and bilateral agreements creates friction.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** 11 million patients, 24M encounters, 35M conditions, 408M observations, 75M claims, 42M prescriptions in b.well's platform; ~1/3 of PA-approved prescriptions never picked up; 82% of physicians say PA can lead to treatment abandonment (AMA 2024 survey); 29% of physicians report PA contributed to a serious adverse event.
- **Studies or research referenced:** September 2025 Johns Hopkins systematic review in The American Journal of Medicine (25 U.S. studies): PA delays associated with preventable hospitalization, disease exacerbation, prolonged hospital stays, and lower cancer survival; AMA 2024 physician survey on PA burdens.
- **Examples provided:** adalimumab, ustekinumab, tocilizumab as biologics existing in both Part B and Part D forms; CARIN Alliance Consumer RTPBC FHIR IG published 2020; NCPDP Foundation grant June 2025 harmonizing consumer/provider RTPB standards; HTI-2 proposals withdrawn December 2025 as reference for ONC certification path.
