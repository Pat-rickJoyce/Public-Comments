# Comment Analysis Template: CMS-2026-1255-0001

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Marva Steed.
- **Stakeholder type:** Individual; Patient.
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Breast cancer patient diagnosed in 2022. The commenter reports denial of a PET scan and several pathology testing protocols requested by her oncologist and references ongoing Signatera testing.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Marva Steed urges CMS to protect oncology patients from prior authorization delays and denials affecting laboratory and biomarker testing. She asks CMS to allow retroactive authorization, prohibit timing-only denials, prevent repeat blood draws, expedite oncology diagnostic review, and protect patients from administrative surprise costs.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** GENERAL; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS by analogy to expedited review for time-sensitive diagnostics; PA-DENIAL-REASONS by analogy to improper timing-based denials.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** No CFR citations are mentioned. The comment references Section III.E of CMS-0062-P, the RFI for Prior Authorization and Access to Laboratory Testing.
- **Standards referenced:** No specific FHIR IGs, NCPDP standards, HL7 standards, or implementation guides are referenced.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Prior Authorization for Oncology Laboratory and Biomarker Testing**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** The commenter states that biomarker and specialty laboratory results guide cancer treatment selection, treatment changes, disease monitoring, and clinical trial eligibility.
- **Specific request:** Protect oncology patients from delays and denials caused by PA requirements for medically necessary laboratory and biomarker testing.

**Topic 2: Retroactive Authorization and Timing-Based Denials**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** The commenter argues that valid specimens may be delayed, rejected, or discarded when rules require authorization before or on the exact specimen collection date, even when all parties acted in good faith.
- **Specific request:** Allow retroactive authorization after specimen collection and prohibit denials based solely on authorization timing.

**Topic 3: Repeat Blood Draws and Specimen Handling**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Repeating blood draws when a valid specimen already exists can delay urgent cancer care and impose avoidable burden on patients.
- **Specific request:** Prevent unnecessary repeat blood draws when a valid specimen is available.

**Topic 4: Expedited Review and Patient Financial Protection**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Cancer care is time-sensitive, and administrative delays can interfere with potentially life-saving treatment decisions. Patients should not bear costs caused by administrative delay.
- **Specific request:** Require expedited review for oncology-related diagnostic and biomarker testing and protect patients from surprise costs when delays are administrative.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S66-S82:** The comment directly responds to the general RFI on prior authorization and access to laboratory testing, with specific emphasis on cancer-related biomarker and specialty laboratory testing.
- **S20/S43:** The comment does not address drug PA timeframes directly, but it supports expedited review for oncology diagnostics where timing can affect treatment decisions.
- **S16:** The comment does not discuss denial explanation language, but it objects to timing-only denials for medically necessary testing.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Allow retroactive authorization when specimens have already been collected.
2. Prohibit denials based solely on authorization timing.
3. Prevent unnecessary repeat blood draws when a valid specimen is available.
4. Require expedited review for oncology-related diagnostic and biomarker testing.
5. Protect patients from surprise costs when delays are administrative.
6. Ensure oncology patients have timely access to laboratory testing needed for treatment decisions.
