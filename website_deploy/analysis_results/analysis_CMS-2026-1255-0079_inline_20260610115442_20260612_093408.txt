ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
As a Medical Science Liaison in migraine, I have engaged in discussions with healthcare professionals across multiple states regarding access to CGRP-targeted therapies. Several healthcare professionals have reported that some patients are unable to obtain coverage for these therapies under certain CMS-related coverage restrictions, despite the recommendations outlined in the American Headache Society (AHS) position statement supporting earlier use of CGRP-targeted treatments in appropriate patients with migraine. According to these healthcare professionals, coverage requirements often necessitate trials of multiple non-specific migraine therapies before approval of a CGRP-targeted treatment, resulting in delays in access to targeted care for some patients.
