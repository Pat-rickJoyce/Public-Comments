## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Joanne Bayouk, MBA, MSN, RN, PMHNP-c / Episode Intelligence Authorization
- **Stakeholder type:** Individual; Technology Vendor / Healthcare policy entrepreneur
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** MBA, MSN, RN, PMHNP-c; author and creator of Episode Intelligence Authorization; developer of CPIG FHIR PlanDefinition and CQL precondition logic.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The commenter submits a companion technical expression for the COB-PA Integrity Gate (CPIG), providing authored CQL variables, MSP trigger logic, FHIR PlanDefinition pseudocode, recovery risk scoring, and episode-level tag persistence. The main policy recommendation is that CMS's FHIR-based prior authorization framework should include a structural payer-order validation gate before clinical criteria evaluation, using FHIR R4 and CQL rather than a claims-stage or advisory COB process.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED; PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS; ONC-STANDARDS-UPDATE; GENERAL
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** 42 CFR Part 411; 42 CFR 411.104; 42 CFR 411.108; 42 CFR 411.40-411.52; 42 CFR 411.162; CMS-0057-F; CMS-2026-1255.
- **Standards referenced:** HL7 FHIR R4 version 4.0.1, FHIRHelpers 4.0.1, Clinical Quality Language, FHIR PlanDefinition, Da Vinci implementation guides, Patient Access API, Provider Access API.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: FHIR/CQL payer-order precondition**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** The comment argues that prior authorization should not proceed to clinical criteria evaluation until MSP triggers and primary payer status are resolved.
- **Specific request:** CMS should adopt or accommodate a FHIR PlanDefinition/CQL precondition gate for primary payer confirmation.

**Topic 2: MSP trigger categories in PA workflow**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** The commenter maps working aged GHP, ESRD coordination period, disability with large GHP, and NGHP cases to specific CQL trigger definitions, arguing these are relevant before authorization decisions are made.
- **Specific request:** CMS should require or encourage structured MSP scenario detection at authorization initiation.

**Topic 3: Episode-level persistence and updates**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL
- **Position:** Clarify
- **Rationale:** The proposed EpisodeMSPRecord and COBChangeLog would persist payer-order status across a clinical episode and re-evaluate when coverage or employment status changes.
- **Specific request:** CMS should clarify how payer-order and COB information should persist across authorization episodes, including drug-related episodes.

**Topic 4: Standards readiness and implementation detail**
- **Provision ID:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS
- **Position:** Support with modifications
- **Rationale:** The comment presents concrete technical logic to show that FHIR/CQL can support enforceable prior authorization transaction rules if FHIR becomes a HIPAA PA standard.
- **Specific request:** CMS/HHS should include testing, implementation, and conformance expectations for precondition logic, not only message exchange.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S1:** The comment supports mandatory use of FHIR-based PA structures by proposing a required PlanDefinition precondition rather than optional guidance.
- **S2:** The comment references FHIR 4.0.1 and FHIRHelpers 4.0.1, implying that version specification matters for executable CQL logic.
- **S3:** The comment does not discuss version transition mechanics, but its use of specific versions indicates that transitions would need to preserve CQL and PlanDefinition compatibility.
- **S12:** The comment identifies a technical gap: current PA workflows lack a FHIR-native MSP payer-order gate before clinical review.
- **S44:** The comment supports FHIR as appropriate for PA standards by providing an example PlanDefinition/CQL architecture.
- **S50:** The comment directly addresses maturity by asserting that the relevant FHIR and CQL infrastructure can express the proposed precondition gate.
- **S54:** The detailed variables, gate decisions, tuples, and routing logic imply a need for testable conformance criteria and certification for PA precondition behavior.
- **S66-S82:** The episode tag and change log concepts respond generally to care coordination and event notification questions by proposing persistent state changes over an authorization episode.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Define a FHIR PlanDefinition precondition that prevents clinical PA evaluation until primary payer status is confirmed when MSP triggers are present.
2. Use structured CQL variables for MSP trigger detection, gate status, payer confirmation, recovery risk, and episode tags.
3. Map MSP scenarios to specific regulatory citations and maintain those mappings in the authorization record.
4. Persist MSP scenario tags and COB change logs across the authorization episode.
5. Route high- and medium-risk MSP scenarios to COB, SIU, or compliance workflows before authorization proceeds.
6. Treat precondition logic as a testable component of FHIR prior authorization conformance.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Existing PA frameworks do not include mandatory payer-order validation; claim-stage COB tools are too late; standards may specify data exchange without requiring workflow gates.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Incorrect payer authorization, MSP recovery exposure, delayed or invalid clinical review, and loss of episode-level tracking when coverage status changes.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Need for FHIR/CQL execution environments, reliable PatientCoverageBundle data, coverage data currency, and defined variables that may not exist directly in current FHIR implementations.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** No quantitative external data are cited in this document.
- **Studies or research referenced:** No studies are cited; the submission provides technical logic and regulatory mapping as support.
- **Examples provided:** Example CQL definitions include WorkingAgedTrigger, ESRDCoordinationTrigger, DisabilityGHPTrigger, NGHPTrigger, HighRiskConditions, MediumRiskConditions, CPIG_GateDecision, and EpisodeMSPRecord.

## 9. IMPACT ASSESSMENT

- **Affected stakeholders:** Payers, COB teams, utilization management teams, SIU/compliance staff, providers, patients with multiple coverage sources, FHIR implementers, and certification/testing entities.
- **Economic implications:** The approach is intended to reduce recovery risk, rework, and incorrect payment responsibility by acting before claims are generated.
- **Technical feasibility:** The comment asserts feasibility through FHIR R4 PlanDefinition and CQL, though some proposed data elements may require extensions or implementation guidance.
- **Patient care impact:** The proposed gate could prevent invalid authorizations, but if implemented poorly, closed gates or 72-hour holds could also delay clinical review.

## 10. SENTIMENT ANALYSIS

- **Overall position:** Supportive of FHIR-based prior authorization with proposed enhancements.
- **Tone:** Technical, proprietary, assertive, and implementation-focused.
- **Priority level:** High
