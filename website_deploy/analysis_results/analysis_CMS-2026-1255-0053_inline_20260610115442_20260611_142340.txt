Current prior authorization rules often create unnecessary barriers that delay or prevent patients from receiving medically necessary testing. These delays can lead to postponed diagnoses, rejected specimens, repeat blood draws, and added stress for people facing cancer or other serious health conditions. CMS has proposed new rules to modernize prior authorization processes and is specifically seeking feedback on challenges related to laboratory testing and authorization requirements. This presents an important opportunity to advocate for patient-centered policies, including eliminating timing-based denials, reducing unnecessary repeat procedures, and accelerating access to time-sensitive diagnostic tests. We are urging CMS to protect patients from administrative delays and financial penalties so they can receive the care they need without avoidable obstacles....
