# Comment Analysis Template: CMS-2026-1255-0001

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Tribal Biotechnology & Health Consortium, submitted by Clear Health Pass Holdings, LLC as Authorized Tribal Agent; signed by John Michael Cataldi, CEO.
- **Stakeholder type:** Government/Other
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Submission asserts formal Government-to-Government status under Executive Order 13175 and HHS/CMS Tribal Consultation Policies, on behalf of ECHAGA Corporation of the Pine Ridge Oglala Sioux Tribe and Blue Lake Rancheria Economic Development Corporation.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Consortium requests that CMS address tribal consultation obligations before finalizing CMS-0062-P and ensure prior authorization criteria do not disadvantage FDA-regulated, tribally owned, or I/T/U-furnished products. It also asks CMS to require disaggregated public PA metrics for AI/AN beneficiaries, I/T/U providers, and tribally owned products, confirm consistency with the Indian Health Care Improvement Act and federal trust responsibility, and respond in the final rule preamble.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** REGULATORY-AUTHORITY/GLOBAL; PA-DENIAL-REASONS; API-USAGE-METRICS; API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING by implication; PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; GENERAL.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** Executive Order 13175; HHS and CMS Tribal Consultation Policies; Administrative Procedure Act; Indian Health Care Improvement Act; federal trust responsibility; treaty obligations; CMS-0062-P.
- **Standards referenced:** FDA status and Medicare coverage; no FHIR IG or NCPDP standards named.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Tribal Consultation Before Finalization**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify/Modify
- **Rationale:** The Consortium frames the rule as implicating Government-to-Government consultation duties and tribal rights.
- **Specific request:** CMS should state on the record whether formal G2G tribal consultation occurred and conduct such consultation before finalizing the rule.

**Topic 2: Anti-Discrimination in Prior Authorization Criteria**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS; PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** PA criteria could disadvantage FDA-regulated, tribally owned, or I/T/U-furnished products, including through improper unproven or investigational designations.
- **Specific request:** CMS should add an express anti-discrimination provision barring such criteria and barring designations inconsistent with FDA status or Medicare coverage.

**Topic 3: Disaggregated Prior Authorization Metrics**
- **Provision ID:** API-USAGE-METRICS; GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Disaggregated metrics would allow CMS and the public to detect differential PA approval and denial patterns affecting AI/AN beneficiaries, I/T/U providers, and tribally owned products.
- **Specific request:** CMS should require public reporting of PA approval and denial metrics disaggregated for these populations and products.

**Topic 4: Consistency with IHCIA and Federal Trust Responsibility**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify
- **Rationale:** The Consortium reserves rights under federal law and seeks confirmation that the rule aligns with tribal health statutes and the trust responsibility.
- **Specific request:** CMS should confirm consistency with the Indian Health Care Improvement Act and federal trust responsibility and respond to the comment in the final rule preamble.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S16:** The comment addresses denial reasons indirectly by seeking to bar denial rationales or PA criteria that label products unproven or investigational when inconsistent with FDA status or Medicare coverage.
- **S29:** The commenter asks CMS to require PA approval and denial metrics disaggregated for AI/AN beneficiaries, I/T/U providers, and tribally owned products.
- **S35:** The request for public disaggregated reporting relates to how CMS should publish or use aggregate metrics, with emphasis on subgroup visibility.
- **S52:** The comment raises legal authority and procedural obligations, including Executive Order 13175, the Indian Health Care Improvement Act, federal trust responsibility, APA rights, and treaty obligations.
- **S66-S82:** The tribal consultation, anti-discrimination, and equity-reporting requests fall within broader general policy and equity RFIs.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. State on the record whether formal G2G tribal consultation was conducted for CMS-0062-P.
2. Conduct tribal consultation before finalizing the rule if it has not occurred.
3. Include an express anti-discrimination provision for FDA-regulated, tribally owned, or I/T/U-furnished products.
4. Bar unproven or investigational designations that conflict with FDA status or Medicare coverage.
5. Require disaggregated public reporting of PA approval and denial metrics for AI/AN beneficiaries, I/T/U providers, and tribally owned products.
6. Confirm consistency with IHCIA and the federal trust responsibility.
7. Respond to the comment in the preamble to the final rule.
