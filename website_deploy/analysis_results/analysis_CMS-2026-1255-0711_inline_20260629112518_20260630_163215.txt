- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Consumers should see price transparency information at the start of prior authorization and at the point of prescribing, rather than having the information limited to utilization management entities and vendors.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES
