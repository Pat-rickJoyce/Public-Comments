It is vital for transparency that Medicare and Medicaid programs be required to report on denials of prescription fills. These denials are often overturned on appeal. Many consumers are receiving denials and do not realize they can appeal. This affects the health status of thousands of Americans. As a former executive of national insurance organizations (CIGNA Healthcare and UnitedHealth Group), the fact that there is no transparency across the 50 states of the metrics around pharmacy management and the resultant denials of prescription now, this must be changed to allow consumers and policy leaders to address the real problems we have in pharmacy administration.
