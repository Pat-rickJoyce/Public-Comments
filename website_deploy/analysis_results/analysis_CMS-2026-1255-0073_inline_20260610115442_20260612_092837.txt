ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
I am a practicing GYN oncologist and would like to express support for the proposed rule to improve the electronic prior authorization processes.

Patients with cancer frequently require urgent access to chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapies, supportive medications, imaging studies, genetic testing, and procedures. The current prior authorization process is often fragmented, time-consuming, and administratively burdensome for both patients and providers. Delays in authorization can postpone treatment initiation, interrupt ongoing therapy, increase patient anxiety, and consume significant clinical resources that could otherwise be directed toward patient care. Practices often hire dedicated personnel to manage prior authorizations and any policy that reduces this burden allows resources to be redirected toward patient care instead of administrative tasks.

I strongly support efforts to standardize and automate prior authorization workflows...
