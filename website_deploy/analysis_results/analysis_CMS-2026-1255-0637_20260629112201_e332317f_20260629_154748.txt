# Comment Analysis: CMS-2026-1255-0637_20260629112201_e332317f

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION
- **Author/Organization name:** Guardant Health
- **Stakeholder type:** Healthcare Provider / Laboratory / Technology-Enabled Diagnostics Company
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Precision oncology company; Guardant360 CDx first FDA-approved liquid biopsy for comprehensive genomic profiling in advanced cancer across solid tumors; more than 1,500 peer-reviewed publications; trusted by more than 15,000 oncologists; over 1,000,000 patient test results; tests covered by Medicare NCDs/LCDs and support more than 100,000 Medicare beneficiaries annually.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Guardant Health responds to the laboratory/DMEPOS RFI by arguing that prior authorization for laboratory testing, especially oncology molecular diagnostics, can delay treatment, create payment denials, and penalize laboratories that process specimens promptly for patient care. It asks CMS to enforce lab-initiated prior authorization rights, protect appeal rights, treat test requisition forms as sufficient documentation when complete, eliminate or streamline PA for NCD/LCD-covered tests, codify exceptions, strengthen MA oversight, and increase accountability for laboratory benefit managers.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT
- **Specific provisions addressed:** GENERAL, PA-DENIAL-REASONS, PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS, API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** 42 CFR 422.566(c)(1)(ii), 42 CFR 422.101(c)(1), 42 CFR 422.101(a), Social Security Act section 1852(a)(1), 42 CFR 405.942, 42 CFR 422.592(a)
- **Standards referenced:** NCDs, LCDs, LCAs, MolDX, DEX registry, CPT coding, TRF/Test Requisition Form, Qualified Independent Contractor (QIC), Independent Review Entity (IRE)

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS
**Topic 1: Laboratories initiating prior authorization**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify / Enforce
- **Rationale:** Labs furnish the service, submit claims, may first identify missing PA, and often know needed documentation; restrictions cause avoidable delays.
- **Specific request:** Enforce compliance with laboratories ability to initiate PA under 42 CFR 422.566(c)(1)(ii).

**Topic 2: Appeal rights and medical necessity review**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Plans classify denials as administrative after PA issues, preventing meaningful Medicare appeals and QIC/IRE review even when the dispute concerns covered medically necessary services.
- **Specific request:** Clarify that claim denials are always appealable on medical necessity grounds regardless of PA outcome and require appropriate forwarding to QIC/IRE.

**Topic 3: Test Requisition Forms as sufficient documentation**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support / Clarify
- **Rationale:** TRFs can include ordering provider attestation, diagnosis, clinical history, and medical necessity information; duplicative record requests add burden and delays.
- **Specific request:** Issue guidance recognizing complete TRFs as valid medical documentation and barring additional record requests when TRF materials are sufficient.

**Topic 4: Coverage criteria consistency across MA and Medicare FFS**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Clarify / Enforce
- **Rationale:** MA plans sometimes impose criteria inconsistent with NCDs/LCDs and related operational materials, limiting access to Medicare-covered molecular diagnostics.
- **Specific request:** Require MA policies to conform to NCDs, LCDs, LCAs, NCD CRs, and contractor databases; establish CMS review for MA restrictions differing from NCD/LCD coverage.

**Topic 5: Eliminate PA for NCD/LCD-covered laboratory tests**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Tests with established Medicare coverage criteria and unique CPT coding already satisfy defined standards; PA adds burden and delay without improving accuracy.
- **Specific request:** Eliminate or reduce PA for lab services covered under NCDs/LCDs; at minimum implement streamlined and automated approval pathways.

**Topic 6: Codify PA exceptions**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Existing FAQ/preamble language is not always accepted in appeals and plans continue requiring PA for out-of-network MA PPO services.
- **Specific request:** Add regulatory text stating PA is not permitted for out-of-network MA PPO covered services and create escalation pathways.

**Topic 7: Oversight, transparency, and enforcement**
- **Provision ID:** API-USAGE-METRICS / GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Current public data and complaint processes do not capture administrative denials, unresolved claims, or systemic lab PA issues.
- **Specific request:** Collect granular denial, overturn, appeal, timing, administrative-denial, and unresolved-claim metrics by service/CPT; create provider-specific reporting and escalation mechanisms; consider Star Ratings or penalties for patterns of inappropriate denials.

**Topic 8: Laboratory Benefit Manager accountability**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** LBMs delegated by MA plans create opaque policies, inconsistent coverage, and additional barriers; delegation should not reduce MA plan accountability.
- **Specific request:** Evaluate LBMs, require transparency, and hold MA plans accountable for delegated utilization management activities.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES
- **S66-S82:** Directly responds to the RFI on laboratory tests and DMEPOS, emphasizing oncology diagnostic testing, laboratory workflows, documentation, appeals, MA oversight, and LBM accountability.
- **S16-S17:** Addresses denial specificity and appealability by requesting medical-necessity review and QIC/IRE forwarding rather than administrative categorization.
- **S29-S35:** Supports stronger, granular oversight metrics for denials, appeals, overturn rates, administrative categorizations, turnaround times, unresolved claims, and CPT-level reporting.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Enforce laboratories ability to initiate prior authorization requests.
2. Protect appeal rights and ensure claim denials remain appealable on medical necessity grounds.
3. Clarify prior authorization requests may be submitted any time until the claim is received and allow retroactive PA with justification.
4. Recognize complete Test Requisition Forms as sufficient medical documentation.
5. Ensure MA coverage criteria conform to NCDs, LCDs, LCAs, NCD CRs, and contractor materials.
6. Eliminate or reduce PA for laboratory services explicitly covered under NCDs/LCDs or with positive MolDX technical assessment and clear CPT coding.
7. Codify existing exceptions, including out-of-network MA PPO services not being subject to PA.
8. Strengthen MA oversight, transparency, provider escalation pathways, enforcement, and granular reporting.
9. Increase accountability and transparency for LBMs and hold MA plans accountable for delegated entities.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS
- **Primary concerns:** PA delays cancer testing and treatment decisions.; Labs are penalized for processing specimens promptly rather than waiting for authorization.; Administrative denial categorizations block appeal rights.; TRF information is ignored in favor of duplicative records.; MA plans and LBMs use criteria inconsistent with Medicare coverage.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Delayed molecular diagnostics, delayed treatment selection, missed targeted therapies, reimbursement disputes, surprise/provider financial burden, unnecessary appeals, patient harm in oncology.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Ordering clinicians may not handle PA promptly, specimens are already collected, labs lack direct access to full medical records, appeal windows are short, LBM policies are opaque, CMS complaint processes are claim-specific and ineffective for systemic issues.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE
- **Data or statistics cited:** More than 1,500 peer-reviewed publications for Guardant tests.; More than 15,000 oncologists trust Guardant tests.; Over 1,000,000 patient test results delivered.; More than 100,000 Medicare beneficiaries supported annually.; More than half of Medicare beneficiaries are enrolled in MA.; Multiple MA organizations request additional documentation for 85% of Shield blood test claims.
- **Studies or research referenced:** California 2021 law prohibiting PA for biomarker testing in advanced cancer.; MolDX Local Coverage Article A59741/A59792 on requisition forms as sufficient documentation when complete.; OIG concerns about inappropriate MA denials referenced indirectly.
- **Examples provided:** Humana Liquid Biopsy MA policy requiring tissue-based CGP infeasibility contrary to NCD 90.2, according to commenter.; Shield colorectal cancer screening claims where age and ICD-10 code establish relevant criteria.; Administrative denials following voided/cancelled PA requests.

## 9. IMPACT ASSESSMENT
- **Affected stakeholders:** Medicare Advantage beneficiaries, Cancer patients, Laboratories, Ordering physicians, Oncologists, MA plans, LBMs, CMS, QIC/IRE appeal entities
- **Economic implications:** PA and duplicative documentation shift financial risk to laboratories, create unreimbursed medically necessary testing, increase appeals costs, and may waste health resources when denials are later overturned.
- **Technical feasibility:** Automation could help if transparent, aligned with Medicare criteria, and paired with oversight; for many covered tests CMS could reduce burden by eliminating PA rather than digitizing it.
- **Patient care impact:** High; delays in advanced cancer molecular testing can postpone treatment selection and targeted therapy initiation.
