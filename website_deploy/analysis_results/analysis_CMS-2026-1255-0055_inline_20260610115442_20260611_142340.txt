As a Maternal-Fetal Medicine physician, I strongly support CMS&rsquo;s efforts to reform prior authorization requirements for diagnostic laboratory testing.<br/><br/>In high-risk obstetrics, timely access to prenatal genetic screening and diagnostic testing is critical. Current prior authorization practices often delay medically necessary testing, force repeat blood draws, and create unnecessary stress for pregnant patients. Denying coverage because authorization was pending on the day a specimen was collected serves no clinical purpose and can delay important medical decisions.<br/><br/>I encourage CMS to:<br/><br/>&bull; Prohibit timing-based denials when testing is medically necessary.<br/>&bull; Eliminate unnecessary repeat blood draws caused by administrative delays.<br/>&bull; Require expedited review pathways for prenatal, oncology, and other time-sensitive diagnostic testing.<br/>&bull; Protect patients and providers from financial penalties resulting from insurance processing delays.<br/><br/>These reforms would improve patient care, reduce administrative burden, and help ensure that patients receive timely access to medically necessary testing.<br/><br/>Thank you for your consideration.<br/><br/>Sincerely,<br/><br/>Nima Goharkhay, MD, PHD<br/>Maternal-Fetal Medicine...
