**2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
The commenter strongly advocates for mandatory electronic prior authorization (ePA) for all pharmacy benefit managers in Medicare and Medicaid programs, eliminating fax-based processes. They emphasize the need for digital communication of outcomes, clinical policies, denial rationales, and all PA responses to reduce paperwork burden.

**Topic: Mandatory Electronic Prior Authorization**
- Position: Support with strengthening
- Rationale: Current paper-based and fax-based systems create excessive administrative burden
- Specific request: Require all PBMs (Medicare, Medicaid) to accept only ePA submissions with no fax option

**Topic: Transparency in PA Decisions**
- Position: Support
- Rationale: Providers need access to clinical policies and specific denial references to understand decisions
- Specific request: Require PBMs to provide outcomes, clinical policy access, and specific denial rationale references through ePA

**Topic: Digital Communication of All PA Responses**
- Position: Support
- Rationale: Eliminate paper burden throughout the PA process
- Specific request: All additional information requests, denials, and approvals should be communicated via ePA response

**5. RECOMMENDATIONS**

2. Eliminate fax as an acceptable submission method for prior authorizations
3. Require digital communication of all PA outcomes through ePA systems
4. Require PBMs to provide access to clinical policies used in determinations
5. Require specific references identifying which criteria were used for denials
