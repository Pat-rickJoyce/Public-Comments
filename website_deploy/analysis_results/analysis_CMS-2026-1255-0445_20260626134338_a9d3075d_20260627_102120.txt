## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** National Hispanic Health Foundation (NHHF).
- **Stakeholder type:** Advocacy Group.
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** NHHF states that it is dedicated to improving health outcomes for Hispanic communities.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

NHHF strongly supports CMS's proposed rule because it would improve access to medicines and therapies, increase transparency, and reduce administrative burden for physicians and Hispanic patients. The comment emphasizes that prior authorization delays and treatment abandonment are especially harmful for Hispanic patients with chronic conditions and for Hispanic Medicare Advantage enrollees.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** GENERAL; PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; TRANSPARENCY-related provisions including API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING and PA-DENIAL-REASONS at a high level.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** None.
- **Standards referenced:** None.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Support for prior authorization reform for drugs**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; GENERAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Prior authorization requirements delay necessary care, contribute to treatment abandonment, and negatively affect clinical outcomes.
- **Specific request:** Finalize reforms that improve the prior authorization process and patient access to essential medicines and therapies.

**Topic 2: Hispanic patient access and health outcomes**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Hispanic patients have higher rates of chronic conditions requiring timely medication access, and many Hispanic Medicare beneficiaries are enrolled in Medicare Advantage plans with extensive PA requirements.
- **Specific request:** Use the rule to reduce access barriers and improve outcomes for Hispanic patients.

**Topic 3: Transparency and administrative burden**
- **Provision ID:** API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING; PA-DENIAL-REASONS; GENERAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** The commenter identifies transparency and reduced physician burden as key benefits of the proposal.
- **Specific request:** Maintain rule provisions that increase transparency and reduce administrative burden.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

No specific numbered solicitation is directly addressed. The comment provides general support relevant to the drug prior authorization provisions and general/RFI topics on patient access, transparency, and burden.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Finalize reforms to improve drug prior authorization processes.
2. Preserve provisions that increase transparency.
3. Reduce administrative burden for physicians and patients.
4. Consider the disproportionate impact of PA delays on Hispanic patients and Medicare Advantage enrollees.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Prior authorization delays, treatment disruption, treatment abandonment, and adverse clinical outcomes.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Interruptions in access to medications for Hispanic patients with diabetes, kidney disease, obesity, and other chronic conditions.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Rigorous prior authorization requirements in Medicare Advantage.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** 95% of physicians say PA delays necessary care; almost 80% report PA challenges have caused treatment abandonment; over nine in 10 report PA negatively affects clinical outcomes; nearly seven in 10 Hispanic Medicare beneficiaries are enrolled in Medicare Advantage; in 2024, 99% of Medicare Advantage enrollees faced PA requirements for some services.
- **Studies or research referenced:** None named.
- **Examples provided:** Hispanic patients with chronic conditions needing consistent access to medications; Medicare Advantage enrollees facing broad PA requirements.

Dear Administrator Oz,  
 
The National Hispanic Health Foundation (NHHF) thanks you for the opportunity to 
comment on CMS's proposed rule to improve interoperability standards and reform the 
prior authorization process. 
 
NHHF is dedicated to improving health outcomes for Hispanic communities. That's why 
we strongly support efforts to improve patient access to essential medicines and therapies. 
CMS's proposed rule would do just that, improving the prior authorization process, 
increasing transparency, and reducing the administrative burden for physicians and 
Hispanic patients. 
 
Prior authorization requirements frequently impede or disrupt necessary care. These 
requirements force physicians to obtain insurer approval before administering a particular 
drug or procedure, often creating severe delays in care. Ninety-five percent of physicians 
say that prior authorization delays access to necessary care. Almost 80% report that prior 
authorization challenges have caused patients to abandon treatment. And over nine in 10 
report that prior authorization negatively impacts clinical outcomes.  
 
Prior authorization barriers are particularly significant for Hispanic patients. Hispanics 
face higher rates of chronic conditions -- such as diabetes, kidney disease, and obesity -- 
that necessitate consistent, timely access to medications. Prior authorization requirements 
often disrupt that access, leaving patients without essential medications and putting their 
health at risk.
