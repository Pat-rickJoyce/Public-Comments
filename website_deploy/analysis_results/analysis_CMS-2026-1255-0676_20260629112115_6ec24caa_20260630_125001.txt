# Comment Analysis Template: CMS-2026-1255-0001

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Health Innovation Alliance (HIA); signed by Roslyn Docktor, Executive Director
- **Stakeholder type:** Advocacy Group / Multi-stakeholder coalition
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Coalition of patient advocates, health care providers, consumer organizations, employers, technology companies, and payers focused on data and technology to improve outcomes and lower costs.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

HIA strongly supports CMS's proposal to modernize prior authorization for drugs using interoperable, standards-based technologies. The comment endorses point-of-care transparency, expansion of the Prior Authorization API to medical benefit drugs, use of NCPDP standards for pharmacy benefit drugs, and cybersecurity coordination that avoids duplicative requirements.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; FHIR-IG-REQUIRED; PA-DENIAL-REASONS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS; STAKEHOLDER-COORDINATION/GENERAL RFI on cybersecurity and trust frameworks.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** No CFR sections cited directly. The comment references CMS-0062-P, CMS-0057-F, the 2024 Interoperability and Prior Authorization final rule, and Medicare Part D/MA-PD ePA rules.
- **Standards referenced:** FHIR Da Vinci; NCPDP SCRIPT; Formulary and Benefit (F&B); Real-Time Prescription Benefit (RTPB); nationally recognized interoperability standards.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Point-of-Care Coverage Transparency**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Actionable coverage, formulary, utilization management, step therapy, alternative therapy, and documentation information in clinical and prescribing workflows can reduce avoidable PA requests and treatment delays.
- **Specific request:** CMS should continue improving the availability and usability of coverage information at the point of care and support embedding F&B and RTPB data in EHR/e-prescribing workflows.

**Topic 2: Medical Benefit Drug Prior Authorization API**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Medical benefit drugs are often clinically significant and time-sensitive, and manual PA processes contribute to interruptions and burden.
- **Specific request:** Finalize the proposal to incorporate medical benefit drugs into the existing Prior Authorization API framework.

**Topic 3: Pharmacy Benefit Drug ePA Standards**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** A consistent approach using NCPDP SCRIPT, F&B, and RTPB can simplify implementation for providers, plans, and developers and align with existing federal rules and adoption patterns.
- **Specific request:** Finalize electronic prior authorization requirements for pharmacy benefit drugs across additional federal healthcare programs.

**Topic 4: Implementation and Cybersecurity Coordination**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify / Clarify
- **Rationale:** Implementation should balance urgency with operational readiness, while cybersecurity expectations should be risk-based, coordinated, and non-duplicative.
- **Specific request:** Provide clear guidance, stakeholder coordination, consistent implementation approaches, and coordination with ONC, OCR, and other federal partners.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S12:** Supports expanding the FHIR-based Prior Authorization API to medical benefit drugs and notes that existing standards-based capabilities can improve documentation exchange and timeliness.
- **S14:** Supports applying pharmacy benefit ePA requirements across additional federal programs using NCPDP standards.
- **S21:** Supports timely implementation but emphasizes adequate lead time, guidance, and coordination.
- **S22:** Suggests that using adopted NCPDP standards and existing investments can reduce implementation complexity.
- **S25:** Supports requiring F&B and RTPB because they provide upstream coverage visibility before a PA request is initiated.
- **S28:** Supports alignment with existing Part D and MA-PD ePA rules and standards.
- **S66-S82:** Responds generally to RFI themes on cybersecurity/trust frameworks by recommending risk-based, coordinated federal expectations.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Finalize expansion of the Prior Authorization API to medical benefit drugs.
2. Finalize pharmacy benefit ePA requirements using NCPDP SCRIPT, F&B, and RTPB.
3. Ensure point-of-care coverage and utilization management information is available in existing clinical and prescribing workflows.
4. Provide adequate lead time, clear implementation guidance, and stakeholder coordination.
5. Coordinate cybersecurity expectations across CMS, ONC, OCR, and other federal partners while minimizing duplicative requirements.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Administrative burden, treatment delays, inconsistent workflows, and potential duplicative cybersecurity/compliance requirements.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Manual PA processes can delay therapy initiation, interrupt treatment, and burden providers and patients.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Need for adequate lead time, clear guidance, coordination, consistent implementation, and support for organizations of varying size and technical capability.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** None.
- **Studies or research referenced:** None.
- **Examples provided:** Examples include point-of-care visibility into prior authorization requirements, coverage limitations, step therapy, formulary status, alternative covered therapies, and documentation requirements.
