## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Zelo Health Inc.; Simon Lorenz, CEO.
- **Stakeholder type:** Technology Vendor; Consumer App Developer.
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** CMS Health Tech Ecosystem early adopter in Conversational AI Assistants; builds a patient/caregiver app using FHIR APIs to aggregate health records and coordinate multi-medication care for older adults.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Zelo Health strongly supports extending prior authorization interoperability to drugs and adopting FHIR R4 for prior authorization transactions. It emphasizes caregiver visibility, centralized endpoint discovery, and making PA status available to patient-authorized applications.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; FHIR-IG-REQUIRED; API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING; HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS; PATIENT-ACCESS-related GENERAL.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** None.
- **Standards referenced:** FHIR APIs; FHIR R4; patient-authorized API access.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: FHIR Drug Prior Authorization**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** FHIR-accessible PA status closes a visibility gap for caregivers managing medications.
- **Specific request:** Finalize FHIR R4 as the PA transaction standard.

**Topic 2: Endpoint Registry and App Access**
- **Provision ID:** API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING
- **Position:** Support/Clarify
- **Rationale:** Without common standards and discoverable endpoints, each payer integration is bespoke and consumer apps cannot reliably access PA data.
- **Specific request:** Require standardized API endpoints and a centralized payer endpoint registry.

**Topic 3: Patient-Authorized PA Status**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Caregivers need to see whether medication PA is approved, pending, or denied before therapy gaps occur.
- **Specific request:** Require PA status availability to patient-authorized applications, not only providers and payers.

**Topic 4: Decision Deadlines**
- **Provision ID:** PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Older adults on multiple chronic medications are harmed by PA delays.
- **Specific request:** Finalize meaningful urgent and standard deadlines.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S23/S24/S33:** Supports standardized endpoints and a centralized payer endpoint registry for patient-access applications.
- **S44:** Supports FHIR R4 as the PA transaction standard.
- **S47:** Supports 24-hour urgent and 72-hour standard decision deadlines as meaningful for older adults and caregivers.
- **S66-S82:** Responds generally that patient-authorized applications should receive PA status through FHIR APIs.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Finalize FHIR R4 as the prior authorization standard.
2. Extend PA interoperability requirements to drugs.
3. Require standardized API endpoints and a centralized payer endpoint registry.
4. Make PA status available to patient-authorized applications.
5. Preserve meaningful urgent and standard PA decision deadlines.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Lack of caregiver visibility into PA status and bespoke payer integrations.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Pharmacy counter surprises, therapy gaps, and harm to older adults on multiple medications.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Lack of common FHIR standard and discoverable endpoint directory.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** None.
- **Studies or research referenced:** None.
- **Examples provided:** Caregivers learning about PA delays only when a prescription is denied at the pharmacy counter.

To the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services,

Zelo Health Inc. is a CMS Health Tech Ecosystem early adopter in the Conversational AI Assistants category. We build a patient and caregiver facing application that aggregates a person's health records through FHIR APIs and helps families coordinate care, including managing complex, multi-medication regimens for older adults.
