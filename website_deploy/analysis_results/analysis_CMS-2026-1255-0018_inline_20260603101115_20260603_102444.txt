ORIGINAL COMMENT (COMPLETE)

To the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services,

I am writing to support the proposed rule regarding Interoperability Standards and Prior Authorization for Drugs. I believe this proposal is a vital step toward modernizing the administrative framework for Medicare and Medicaid, specifically by reducing the inefficiencies associated with the current prior authorizations process with prescription drugs.

One part of the proposal I especially agree with is the focus of making health information easier to share between providers, insurers, and patients. Many patients experience delays, repeated paperwork, or duplicate testing because different healthcare systems are not connected. Better interoperability could improve coordination between providers and help patients receive faster and more informed care.

I also believe this proposal could help reduce information gaps that currently exist between patients, providers, and insurance companies. Patients often do not have full access to or understanding of their own medical records when making treatment decisions. Improving access to accurate and updated information can help reduce these asymmetries and lead to better healthcare decisions overall.

In addition, improving data sharing may help reduce unnecessary costs caused by duplicate tests, billing mistakes, and administrative inefficiencies. These improvements could benefit both patients and healthcare programs over time. At the same time, I believe strong privacy and cybersecurity protections should remain a priority as more health information becomes digitally connected. Expanding access to healthcare data should also include clear accountability standards to protect sensitive patient information.

Overall, I believe this proposal is a positive step toward improving transparency, coordination, and efficiency within the healthcare system.

Thank you
