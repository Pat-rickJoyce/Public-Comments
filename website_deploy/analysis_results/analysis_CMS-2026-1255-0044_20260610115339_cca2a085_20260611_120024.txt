## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The commenter strongly supports CMS-0062-P and the expansion of interoperability standards and electronic prior authorization for drugs. She emphasizes benefits for older adults and caregivers and asks CMS to finalize the proposal while continuing to focus on usability and implementation support for providers of all sizes, including smaller practices and organizations serving older adults.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; FHIR-IG-REQUIRED; GENERAL.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** CMS-0062-P.
- **Standards referenced:** Standards-based interoperability; electronic prior authorization; secure information exchange. No specific FHIR IGs or NCPDP standards named.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Overall support for electronic PA and interoperability**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Modernized electronic PA can reduce burden, speed decisions, improve transparency, reduce duplicative documentation, and coordinate across organizations and plans.
- **Specific request:** Finalize CMS-0062-P.

**Topic 2: Older adult and caregiver impact**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Older adults managing chronic conditions are particularly vulnerable to treatment delays, and caregivers spend substantial time navigating PA requirements.
- **Specific request:** Preserve focus on patient and caregiver experience.

**Topic 3: Standards-based secure data movement**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Critical data remain trapped in disconnected systems, limiting patient-centered coordination.
- **Specific request:** Continue emphasizing standards-based interoperability among providers, pharmacies, plans, and patients.

**Topic 4: Usability and implementation support**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Providers of all sizes must be able to adopt new standards without undue burden.
- **Specific request:** Provide usability focus and implementation support, especially for smaller practices and organizations serving older adults.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S1/S18/S37:** The comment generally supports mandatory interoperability standards and requests attention to usability and implementation support.
- **S12/S14:** It supports expansion of electronic PA to drugs, though without distinguishing medical and pharmacy benefit technical requirements.
- **S66-S82:** The comment addresses broader RFI themes of patient/caregiver experience, coordination, and reducing administrative complexity.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Finalize CMS-0062-P.
2. Continue advancing standards-based interoperability and electronic PA for drugs.
3. Ensure secure, efficient information movement among providers, pharmacies, plans, and patients.
4. Focus on usability and implementation support for providers of all sizes.
5. Pay particular attention to smaller practices and organizations serving older adults.
