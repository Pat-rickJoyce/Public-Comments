## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Real-time consumer benefit and cost transparency**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Consumers need real-time, specific benefit and out-of-pocket cost information, including delegated tier pricing, at decision points that affect medication access.
- **Specific request:** Require or support consumer access to real-time benefit cost and tier-pricing information at the start of prior authorization and at prescribing.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S25:** The comment indirectly supports mature real-time prescription benefit functionality by asking for real-time, patient-specific out-of-pocket cost information at prescribing.
- **S31:** The comment may relate to QHP or payer implementation of pharmacy benefit transparency, though it does not identify a payer type.
- **S66-S82:** The comment is a general request for consumer-facing transparency within prior authorization and prescribing workflows.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Provide consumers real-time access to specific benefit out-of-pocket cost information.

3. Make this information available when prior authorization begins and at the point of prescribing.
4. Do not reserve the information solely for delegated utilization management entities.
