# Comment Analysis: CMS-2026-1255-0091

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** Medical Information Technology, Inc. (MEDITECH).
- **Stakeholder type:** Technology Vendor.
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** EHR vendor offering Expanse in 29 countries and territories, with AI, cloud, and interoperability capabilities.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

MEDITECH supports adoption of core FHIR specifications for prior authorization-related eligibility transactions but warns that versioning, CDex attachments, and simultaneous implementation timelines could create substantial vendor and provider burden. It recommends using Da Vinci Burden Reduction IG 2.2.n versions, avoiding reliance on minor-version updates for breaking changes, deferring CDex requirements, and aligning CMS and ONC version requirements and timelines.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS; FHIR-IG-REQUIRED; ONC-STANDARDS-UPDATE; STANDARDS-MATURITY; GENERAL.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** Sections II.H.6, II.H.7, II.H.8, II.H.10, and II.A.5 of the proposed rule; no CFR citations.
- **Standards referenced:** HL7 FHIR R4 4.0.1; US Core IG STU 6.1.0; SMART App Launch 2.0.0; CRD IG 2.2.1; Da Vinci Burden Reduction IGs; CDex IG 2.1.0; FAST Security IG; Tiered OAuth; ATR List; TEFCA; CMS Aligned Network.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: FHIR PA standards adoption and versions**
- **Provision ID:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS / FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Support/Modify
- **Rationale:** MEDITECH supports FHIR specifications for eligibility transactions used to determine whether PA is required, but urges adoption of current 2.2.n Da Vinci Burden Reduction IG versions.
- **Specific request:** Adopt the 2.2.n versions of the Da Vinci Burden Reduction IGs and the listed FHIR specifications.

**Topic 2: Versioning and breaking changes**
- **Provision ID:** ONC-STANDARDS-UPDATE
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** MEDITECH states that minor versions of Da Vinci Burden Reduction IGs have included breaking changes, so HL7's Major.Minor.Patch convention is unreliable unless enforced consistently.
- **Specific request:** Do not treat minor version updates as automatic maintenance updates if they include breaking changes.

**Topic 3: CDex attachments**
- **Provision ID:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS
- **Position:** Oppose/Defer
- **Rationale:** CDex has multiple workflows, is large to implement, and production experience with burden reduction workflows is limited.
- **Specific request:** Recommend rather than require CDex, clarify use cases, and focus first on refining CRD, DTR, and PAS workflows.

**Topic 4: Compliance timeline and vendor feasibility**
- **Provision ID:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** EHR vendors cannot economically implement CDex and Burden Reduction IGs on the same timeline.
- **Specific request:** Phase CDex, or do not add it, and ensure CMS and ONC adopt identical versions and timelines.

**Topic 5: Recommended IG maturity and security**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED / PRIVACY-SECURITY
- **Position:** Clarify/Modify
- **Rationale:** MEDITECH says current recommended IGs are not mature enough, except FAST Security is closest but depends on community trust infrastructure.
- **Specific request:** Replace ATR List with a record locator service; consider CDex only after burden reduction issues are resolved; adopt FAST Security when TEFCA and a payer community can manage certificates.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES

- **S1:** Says none of the currently recommended IGs are mature enough to require now, with FAST Security closest to maturity.
- **S2:** Recommends adopting the 2.2.n versions of Da Vinci Burden Reduction IGs.
- **S3/S60:** Opposes automatic reliance on Major.Minor.Patch unless minor versions cannot contain breaking changes.
- **S44/S45:** Supports adopting listed FHIR standards for PA-related eligibility transactions, with limits and version clarity.
- **S47/S53:** Warns implementation is feasible only with aligned CMS/ONC versions and timelines and with CDex phased or deferred.
- **S48/S49:** States implementing CDex and Burden Reduction simultaneously would not be economical for vendors and covered entities.
- **S50:** Argues Burden Reduction and CDex workflows lack sufficient production maturity.
- **S54:** Implies more testing and production feedback are needed before adding new IGs.
- **S66-S82:** Addresses RFI topics on FAST Security, Tiered OAuth, ATR List, and scalable authentication.

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Adopt the 2.2.n Da Vinci Burden Reduction IG versions.
2. Do not rely on minor-version updates as maintenance changes if breaking changes are possible.
3. Do not require CDex now; clarify use cases and consider it after CRD, DTR, and PAS mature in production.
4. Do not implement CDex and Burden Reduction IGs on the same timeline.
5. Align CMS and ONC version requirements and compliance dates exactly.
6. Replace ATR List concepts with a record locator service.
7. Consider FAST Security when TEFCA and payer-community certificate management are available.
8. Use Tiered OAuth for user authentication across APIs where individual users need access.

## 7. CONCERNS & OBJECTIONS

- **Primary concerns:** Breaking changes in minor versions, immature IGs, CDex scope and burden, simultaneous large implementation projects, CMS/ONC misalignment.
- **Potential negative impacts cited:** Increased burden, implementation errors, uneconomical vendor work, provider burden from additional standards.
- **Implementation barriers mentioned:** Four CDex workflows, limited production use, large IG implementation scope, lack of payer community certificate infrastructure.

## 8. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

- **Data or statistics cited:** MEDITECH operates in 29 countries and territories.
- **Studies or research referenced:** None.
- **Examples provided:** Minor versions of Da Vinci Burden Reduction IGs have had breaking changes; CDex has four workflows; ATR lists may be immediately outdated.

## 9. IMPACT ASSESSMENT

- **Affected stakeholders:** EHR vendors, providers, HIPAA covered entities, payers, CMS, ONC, HL7, TEFCA participants.
- **Economic implications:** MEDITECH says simultaneous CDex and Burden Reduction implementation would not be economical.
- **Technical feasibility:** Feasible only with stable versions, aligned timelines, and phased scope.
- **Patient care impact:** Indirect; reduced implementation errors and burden may improve PA workflow reliability.
