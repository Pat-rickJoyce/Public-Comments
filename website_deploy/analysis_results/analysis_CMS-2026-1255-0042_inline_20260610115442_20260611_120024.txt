ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
CMS is proposing a rule to improve the exchange and use of health information, streamline prior authorization processes, and support continuity of care when patients switch health plans. I support this proposed rule because it has the potential to reduce administrative burdens on both patients and healthcare providers. By improving the efficiency of information sharing, the rule could help patients receive timely care and help providers spend less time navigating complex authorization requirements.<br/><br/>If patient information does not transfer smoothly when a person changes health plans, the patient may lose timely access to medically necessary medications or treatments. Improved data sharing and interoperability can help ensure that a patient&#39;s medical history, prior authorizations, and treatment information are available when coverage changes. This can reduce disruptions in care and help patients continue receiving appropriate treatment without unnecessary delays.<br/><br/>Improved sharing of health information between healthcare providers and health plans could help providers make more informed decisions more quickly. When relevant patient information is available through interoperable systems, providers may spend less time requesting records or clarifying coverage requirements. This can reduce administrative burdens, improve efficiency, and allow healthcare professionals to focus more of their time on patient care.<br/><br/>I believe this proposed rule will provide meaningful benefits to patients, healthcare providers, and health plans. By streamlining the exchange of health information and supporting continuity of care, the rule has the potential to improve healthcare delivery and produce positive outcomes for communities. I encourage CMS to move forward with the proposed rule....
