ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
I am a psychology graduate student who works with patients with migraine and other headache disorders. I am submitting this comment about CMS-0062-P. Many of the patients that I see are dealing with psychological distress that exacerbate their headache disorders, and inefficiencies and lack of transparency with CMS have caused unnecessary harm to them. Faster decision timeframes matter to deliver treatment sooner and allow them to experience quicker symptom relief and improve their qualities of life. Aldo, patients who are stable on treatment should not be forced to start over because of insurance churn. CMS should be required to use updated specialty guidelines&nbsp;from the American Headache Society.
