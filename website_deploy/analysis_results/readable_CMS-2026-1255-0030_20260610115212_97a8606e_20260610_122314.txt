Mental health advocacy organization focusing on SMI (schizophrenia, schizoaffective, bipolar, MDD). Supports transparency provisions but urges critical antipsychotic protections. Key recommendations: distinguish procedural vs. substantive denials, extend Part D protections to new starts, protect Part B injectable antipsychotics, require 24-hour expedited exceptions, prohibit automated denials for life-sustaining medications.

Clinical evidence: Antipsychotic switches cause relapse with neurological changes making future treatment less effective. No "equivalent" antipsychotics. Downstream costs (ED, hospitalization, criminal justice) far exceed formulary savings.
