ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
The new rules proposed here create considerable burdens for disabled americans and the families that care for them. As a corporate leader in a multi billion dollar multinational company, we rely on the stability of medicaid and medicare to protect the most fragile Americans. Even worse, if there are difficulties with filing, there is no clear path to find and the regulation to ensure back payment of benefits. This will have a demonstrably negative impact on medical costs. The current regulation as written does not provide the kind of detailed clarity needed to better prepare applicants to submit paperwork and to understand what conditions qualify for exemptions of the work requirement. I would ask the administration to table this rule.Until a real administrative structure can be created....
