# Comment Analysis: CMS-2026-1255-0620

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION
- **Author/Organization name:** AHIP
- **Stakeholder type:** Payer / Professional Association
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** National association representing health insurance plans covering over 205 million Americans across Medicare, Medicaid, employer-sponsored coverage, and the individual market.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
AHIP supports extending electronic prior authorization requirements to drugs, but argues CMS must balance innovation with implementation feasibility and regulatory stability. Its core requests are a 24-month post-final-rule compliance timeline, no premature mandatory IG versions, deferral of endpoint reporting, stronger provider adoption incentives, and narrower, contextualized metrics.

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT
- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL; PA-DRUG-PHARMACY; FHIR-IG-REQUIRED; API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING; API-USAGE-METRICS; PA-DENIAL-REASONS; PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS; HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS; ONC-STANDARDS-UPDATE; FF-SHOP-REQUIREMENTS; GENERAL.
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** 45 CFR 170.215; 5 U.S.C. 706(2)(c); Paperwork Reduction Act; HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules; ONC Health IT Certification Program; MIPS; Promoting Interoperability; Medicare Conditions of Participation.
- **Standards referenced:** FHIR R4; HL7 Da Vinci CRD, DTR, PAS; US Core; CARIN IG for Blue Button; CARIN RTPBC IG; NCPDP standards; X12 278; X12 270/271; CAQH operating rules; FAST Security IG; NDH IG Endpoint Profile; Inferno; SVAP; CDex; TEFCA.

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Drug prior authorization implementation timeline**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL / PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Plans are still implementing non-drug ePA APIs and need time to validate those workflows before adding drug-specific complexities, including medical/pharmacy benefit routing and PBM coordination.
- **Specific request:** Set compliance at 24 months after the final rule effective date instead of October 1, 2027.

**Topic 2: Medical versus pharmacy benefit distinction and unified authorizations**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL / PA-DRUG-PHARMACY
- **Position:** Clarify
- **Rationale:** Providers may not know the correct benefit pathway at ordering, and rigid separation could fragment oncology and other regimen-level care.
- **Specific request:** Clarify routing and permit unified prior authorizations for integrated regimens, protocols, and episodes of care.

**Topic 3: Mandatory FHIR IGs and version management**
- **Provision ID:** FHIR-IG-REQUIRED
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Specific versions, especially Da Vinci CRD/DTR/PAS 2.0, may be outdated or immature, and IGs can impose policy beyond rulemaking.
- **Specific request:** Do not require specific CRD/DTR/PAS versions now; retain a named base FHIR standard; specify applicable IG capabilities by API; provide stable transition rules.

**Topic 4: Provider adoption of ePA**
- **Provision ID:** GENERAL
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Payer investments will not reduce burden unless providers and EHR vendors adopt and use the API in production.
- **Specific request:** Move from attestation to performance-based measures, eventually consider a Medicare CoP, and align EHR certification and incentives with payer API requirements.

**Topic 5: Specific denial reasons**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** There is no single national taxonomy across X12, CAQH, CMS, and proprietary codes; vendors need time for reconfiguration and testing.
- **Specific request:** Set October 1, 2028 compliance, create an SDO-maintained denial reason taxonomy, issue detailed guidance, align with NCQA, and phase implementation where vendor constraints exist.

**Topic 6: Drug PA timeframes**
- **Provision ID:** PA-TIMEFRAMES-DRUGS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Compressed timeframes without complete electronic submissions may increase denials, appeals, and patient safety risks.
- **Specific request:** Apply 24-hour response only for complete ePA submissions; measure timeframes from complete request receipt; allow clock pauses for missing information; make October 1, 2028 the earliest compliance date.

**Topic 7: Payer endpoint reporting**
- **Provision ID:** API-ENDPOINT-REPORTING
- **Position:** Oppose / Defer
- **Rationale:** CMS has not fully explained legal authority, data scope, governance, PRA implications, publication format, validation, security risks, or complex payer hierarchy representation.
- **Specific request:** Do not finalize mandatory endpoint reporting in this rule; issue a comprehensive proposed rule. If CMS proceeds, use NDH Endpoint resources and clarify treatment of brands, affiliates, lines of business, delegates, and state-specific models.

**Topic 8: Prior authorization and API metrics**
- **Provision ID:** API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** Detailed metrics before APIs stabilize may be inconsistent and burdensome; raw counts can mislead because plan size differs.
- **Specific request:** Use narrow initial metrics, maintain percentages rather than raw counts, permit contextual narrative, and delay drug reporting changes until at least 2029 reporting using 2028 data.

**Topic 9: HIPAA prior authorization standards**
- **Provision ID:** HIPAA-PA-STANDARDS
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** FHIR can modernize transactions, but broad HIPAA-covered entity adoption requires clear authority, phased implementation, coexistence rules, and alignment with X12/NCPDP workflows.
- **Specific request:** Finalize a carefully phased FHIR transition for prior authorization, avoid overbroad scope, and provide testing, guidance, and implementation flexibility.

**Topic 10: ONC certification and payer API testing**
- **Provision ID:** ONC-STANDARDS-UPDATE
- **Position:** Modify
- **Rationale:** ONC can support testing, but formal payer certification may exceed statutory authority and create inappropriate information-blocking implications.
- **Specific request:** Support robust voluntary testing outside the statutory certification program, including test tools for all unexpired IG versions, technical assistance, and payer-focused forums.

## 5. SOLICITATION RESPONSES
- **S1-S3, S18, S32, S37:** Supports standardization but opposes premature mandatory versions; requests API-specific capability scoping and coordinated CMS/ONC transitions.
- **S12, S15, S21, S28:** Requests more time and flexibility for drug ePA, especially benefit routing and unified episode-level authorizations.
- **S14, S19, S22, S25, S31:** Requests alignment with NCPDP/PBM infrastructure, EHR vendor readiness, and continued exclusion of FFE stand-alone dental plans.
- **S16-S17:** Supports clear denial reasons with a later date, national taxonomy, NCQA alignment, and guidance.
- **S20, S43:** Opposes broad 24-hour decision timelines absent complete electronic submissions and requests clock pauses and complete-request definitions.
- **S23-S24, S26-S27, S33:** Requests deferral of endpoint reporting pending authority, governance, data scope, privacy/security, and PRA process.
- **S29-S30, S34-S35:** Requests narrow, phased usage metrics with privacy protections and implementation experience first.
- **S44-S56:** Supports modernization but urges careful phasing, scope limits, testing, authority analysis, and coexistence planning.
- **S60-S65:** Requests predictable ONC standards updates and voluntary testing support rather than payer certification mandates.

## 6. THEME TAXONOMY
IMPLEMENTATION-TIMELINE; COST-RESOURCES; PROVIDER-BURDEN; TECHNICAL-FEASIBILITY; STANDARDS-MATURITY; REGULATORY-AUTHORITY; STAKEHOLDER-COORDINATION; EXISTING-WORKFLOWS; VENDOR-ECOSYSTEM; TRANSPARENCY; MEDICAL-VS-PHARMACY-BENEFIT.

## 7. STAKEHOLDER IMPACT
The comment primarily reflects payer concerns but emphasizes impacts on providers, PBMs, EHR vendors, clearinghouses, SDOs, patients, and all HIPAA covered entities if FHIR prior authorization standards are adopted broadly.

## 8. KEY REQUESTS
Set a 24-month post-final-rule compliance timeline, do not require immature or outdated IG versions, defer endpoint reporting, require provider adoption incentives, use narrower metrics, and implement HIPAA/FHIR transitions in phases.

## 9. OVERALL POSITION
Support with significant modifications.
