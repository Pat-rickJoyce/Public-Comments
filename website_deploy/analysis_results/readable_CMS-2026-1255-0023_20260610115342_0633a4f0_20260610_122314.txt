# Comment Analysis: CMS-2026-1255-0001

---

## 1. COMMENTER INFORMATION

- **Author/Organization name:** The Society of Thoracic Surgeons (STS) - Vinay Badhwar, MD, President
- **Stakeholder type:** Professional Association
- **Credentials or expertise mentioned:** Not-for-profit organization representing more than 8,000 surgeons, researchers, and allied health care professionals worldwide dedicated to heart, lung, and esophagus surgery outcomes

---

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

STS supports CMS's proposal to expand Prior Authorization APIs to include drugs covered under medical benefit, require specific denial reasons, and publicly report PA metrics for drugs. The organization emphasizes that PA delays significantly impede patient care and advocates for user-friendly implementation, standardized metrics, and granular stratification by drug class or clinical indication.

---

## 3. REGULATORY CONTEXT

- **Specific provisions addressed:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL, PA-DENIAL-REASONS, API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Regulatory citations mentioned:** 2024 Advancing Interoperability and Improving Prior Authorization Final Rule
- **Standards referenced:** Prior Authorization APIs, public website reporting

---

## 4. DETAILED POSITIONS

**Topic 1: Prior Authorization APIs for Medical Benefit Drugs**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DRUG-MEDICAL
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Improving accessibility and transparency of PA requirements reduces administrative burden and mitigates delays in patient access. National survey data show nearly 90% of physicians report PA delays access to necessary treatment, with many linking delays to worsening clinical outcomes and increased emergency/hospital service use. Analysis found only small share of requests approved immediately, with 44% approved after median 6 days and 45% ultimately denied.
- **Specific request:** Ensure API enhancements are user-friendly and do not inadvertently add complexity for providers.

**Topic 2: Specific Denial Reasons for Drug PA**
- **Provision ID:** PA-DENIAL-REASONS
- **Position:** Support
- **Rationale:** Clear, specific denial rationales improve transparency and address barriers to accessing medically necessary drugs. PA creates administrative burden and delays therapy initiation, contributing to treatment disruption and nonadherence. Standardized, actionable denial explanations are essential for efficiency and fairness.
- **Specific request:** Finalize requirement for specific denial reasons for all drugs.

**Topic 3: Public Reporting of PA Metrics for Drugs**
- **Provision ID:** API-USAGE-METRICS
- **Position:** Support with enhancement
- **Rationale:** Public reporting of approval rates, denial rates, and timeliness provides insight into how utilization management affects access to medically necessary therapies and helps identify variation across payers.
- **Specific request:** (1) Ensure metrics are standardized across payers to allow meaningful comparison; (2) Require more granular stratification where feasible, such as by drug class or clinical indication, to better capture access issues relevant to complex specialty care.

**Implicit responses:**

- **S16 (what is "specific reason" for denials):** Supports standardized, actionable denial explanations
- **S29 (which API usage metrics to collect):** Recommends standardized metrics including approval rates, denial rates, timeliness, with granular stratification by drug class/clinical indication
- **S35 (publish aggregate metrics):** Supports public reporting to identify variation across payers

---

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Ensure PA API enhancements are user-friendly and do not add provider complexity
2. Finalize requirement for specific denial reasons for all drug PA requests
3. Standardize PA metrics across all payers to enable meaningful comparison
4. Require granular stratification of metrics by drug class or clinical indication where feasible
5. Use public transparency to inform future policy efforts aimed at reducing unnecessary delays
