ORIGINAL COMMENT EXCERPT
------------------------
I am writing in strong support of CMS&#39;s proposed rule on prior authorization. Every day, patients are forced to wait weeks or months for treatments their doctors have already prescribed. Prior authorization requirements delay necessary care, drive up costs, and put patients at risk. Doctors, not insurance companies, should decide what care patients need. I urge CMS to finalize and strengthen this rule by requiring 24-hour decisions, mandating clear justifications for all denials, and ensuring a robust appeals process. Patients can&#39;t afford to wait.
